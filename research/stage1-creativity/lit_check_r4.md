2 | PARTIAL | closest work: Jiang, Lim, Yao, Ye, Statistical Ranking and Combinatorial Hodge Theory / HodgeRank (2011) https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.1067 (gradient plus curl plus harmonic split of edge flows) ; Balduzzi et al., Open-ended Learning in Symmetric Zero-sum Games (2019) https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.08106 (transitive vs cyclic split of agent payoffs) ; nearest in RL: Cochain Perspectives on Temporal-Difference Signals (2026) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.06939 | the Hodge split and the point that a scalar rating or value is a pure gradient are known; I found no prior work that builds the cell complex from experienced transitions, solves it from delayed-outcome chain-sum constraints, and keeps persistent non-zero harmonic holes as reusable strategy concepts.
3 | PARTIAL | closest work: Thyagarajan et al., closure-invariant machine learning for source classification and imaging (2023-24) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.06349 (also VLBInet 2021, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.07185) ; Closure statistics in interferometric data (2019) https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.02062, which shows closure-based inference is equivalent to self-calibration with free gains (bispectrum/triple-correlation invariants in recognition, e.g. Kakarala https://arxiv.org/pdf/0902.0196, are the other relative) | the algebra, the ML use of closure quantities and the closure-equals-self-cal link are all known within astronomy; I found nothing outside astronomy that makes it a general recognizer (running-average closure templates, alternating match and closed-form per-source gain solve, returning the corruptions).
4 | PARTIAL | closest work: Baum, Toward a Model of Intelligence as an Economy of Agents (1999) https://dx.doi.org/10.1023/A:1007593124513 (Hayek machine; Schmidhuber's PSALM work enforces credit conservation) ; Waudby-Smith et al., Anytime-valid off-policy inference for contextual bandits (2022) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.10768 (e-processes in bandits and RL, but for policy-value inference, not per-link credit) | conserved wealth routing and e-process testing both exist, but separately; I found no work that tags wealth with tracers, solves mass balance for delayed credit, and gates each link update on its own e-process crossing 1/alpha for a bound on spurious credited links.
5 | KNOWN | closest work: Sharma, Gupta, Hariharan, Aiken, Liang, Nori, A Data Driven Approach for Algebraic Loop Invariants (2013) https://mail.saurabhg.web.illinois.edu/pdfs/sharma2013data.pdf (integer/linear invariants guessed from execution traces by linear algebra, then checked), together with Petri-net P-invariants and the state equation as unreachability proofs ; Fišer & Komenda, Fact-Alternating Mutex Groups for Classical Planning (2018) https://jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/11181/26386 (invariants used for dead-end detection; also arXiv 2105.03096 on inductive half-spaces for Petri-net non-reachability, whose title I could not verify) | the core, integer conserved quantities proving unreachability, is textbook; what remains is smallest-k grade learning inside a neural rewriter with split or raise-k revision, and as far as I found no one combines these (and invariants learned from observed rewrites are only empirical, not proved, unless checked).
NOTE: Four of the five ideas (1, 2, 3, 4) are transplants of a mature mechanism into learning (sheaf obstruction, Hodge decomposition, closure invariants, conserved-credit economies plus e-processes). In each, the part the originating field already has is the diagnostic or algebra, and the part I could not find is the closed loop where that diagnostic drives structure change or learning updates. Idea 5 is mostly already known (Petri nets, planning mutexes, data-driven invariants); the most defensible novelty claims are 1, 2 and 4, where the combination is not published. Searches were shallow (2-4 per idea) and a few arXiv pages could not be fetched, so "not found" does not mean absent.
