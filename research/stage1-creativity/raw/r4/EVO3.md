PAIR 1: Parent A's cell complex becomes the space that Parent B's rewriting moves through. Vertices are program-graph states, edges are rule applications, and a 2-cell records a proven join, where two rewrite paths reach the same normal form. Rewriting builds the topology. The topology then steers rewriting: the homology class a path winds around picks the next rule, replacing the neural prior.
Honesty check: the closest work is Squier, Guiraud–Malbos homotopical completion and Knuth–Bendix. Those fill every hole to force confluence. Mutation: holes that persist across many inputs are kept and promoted to explicit choice variables, and rules are scored by Betti-number reduction per bit, not by compressing experience.

PAIR 2: Walkers carry a hypothesis vector that is transported through the restriction maps. A walker attaches where its accumulated holonomy (its mismatch after a loop) is high, and a new patch is grown there. Screening sends capacity to the contradiction tips that face the most unexplored context.
Honesty check: the closest work is sheaf diffusion, connection Laplacians and Hansen–Ghrist sheaf opinion dynamics. Those smooth disagreement away. This one grows at disagreement, with no Laplacian solve and no information-gain score.

PAIR 3: The wealth is the conserved flow. Routing fractions are each link's bets, and renormalizing at a node is a convex merge of e-values, which keeps them valid. Unmixing the tracers gives every past decision its own e-process across arbitrary delays.
Honesty check: the closest work is online FDR with e-values (e-LOND), universal portfolios and GFlowNets. Mutation: this is a gated learning rule, not a test. A credit enters routing only once its e-process crosses 1/α.

=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Persistent Confluence Rewriter
MECHANISM: The model stores a cell complex over rewrite states: rule applications are edges, and proven joins of two rewrite paths are 2-cells. A query's rewrite path is classified by sparse boundary-matrix algebra, and its homology class picks the next rule. New rules are kept when they kill transient 1-cycles cheaply. Cycles that persist across many inputs become typed choice variables.
DIFFERENCE: It can certify that an answer does not depend on reasoning order, or list exactly the independent choices it does depend on.

IDEA 2
NAME: Holonomy-Seeded Patch Growth
MECHANISM: Random walkers launched from unvisited context carry a hypothesis vector through the learned restriction maps between overlapping local models. Each walker accumulates a holonomy residual, and when that residual crosses a threshold the walker attaches by spawning or splitting a patch at that spot. Because frontier patches screen walkers, growth branches toward the contradictions that face the most unexplored context. The launch rate adapts to new patches per walker.
DIFFERENCE: It decides where to add knowledge from the geometry of its own contradictions, with no learned uncertainty, value or novelty estimator.

IDEA 3
NAME: Certified Wealth-Flow Credit
MECHANISM: Betting wealth is routed conservatively through the units, and each decision tags its share with a tracer. When an outcome arrives, a mass-balance solve gives each past decision its credited wealth. That multiplies the decision's e-process (null hypothesis: the decision had no effect on the outcome). A link's routing fraction is updated only after its e-process exceeds 1/α.
DIFFERENCE: It learns from arbitrarily delayed outcomes while bounding the fraction of credited causal links that are spurious, at any stopping time.
