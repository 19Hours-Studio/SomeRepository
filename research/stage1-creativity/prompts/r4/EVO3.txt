 Parent B: MECHANISM: The model's state is a typed graph of small symbolic programs and data nodes. PAIR 2
 Parent A: MECHANISM: The state is an aggregate of discovered items on a graph of candidate items. Random walkers launched from unexplored territory step between neighbors until they touch the aggregate and attach, adding one item and exposing new frontier. Growth concentrates at tips that screen the most unexplored territory, since walkers reach them first, so the aggregate branches fractally over the reachable space. Launch and attachment rules adapt to the yield of new items per walker.
   DIFFERENCE: It covers an unbounded space in proportion to the unexplored territory each frontier point screens, with no value function or novelty score, which current exploration bonuses must learn.
   nearest published work: frontier/count-based exploration
 Parent B: MECHANISM: Knowledge is stored as thousands of small, separately trained local models. Each one is valid only on an overlapping region of context space (a "patch"), and learned restriction maps say how neighbouring patches must agree where they overlap. Inference searches for a global section: an assignment that satisfies all active patches at once, found by iterative consistency propagation instead of a single forward pass. Learning adds, splits or refits patches only where the measured disagreement (the obstruction to gluing them together) is high, and leaves every other patch untouched.
   DIFFERENCE: It can pinpoint and report exactly where its own knowledge contradicts itself, and it can learn new facts without overwriting old ones because no globally shared weights exist to interfere.
   nearest published work: sheaf neural nets / local-to-global computation (Bosca & Ghrist 2026), LWPR

 Parent A: MECHANISM: The state is a wealth vector of e-processes, one per candidate answer. Each new observation multiplies each candidate's wealth by a likelihood ratio from a learned predictor, and compute is spent on the fastest-growing bets. An answer is committed when its wealth exceeds 1/α, and that error bound holds under any stopping rule.
   DIFFERENCE: It bounds the error of committed answers at any stopping time, which confidence scores and self-consistency voting cannot guarantee.
   nearest published work: anytime-valid LLM stopping rules / testing by betting for self-consistency (2025-2026)
 Parent B: MECHANISM: Signals are a conserved quantity routed between units. Each unit holds the flow-weighted mix of its inflows, and each decision injects a unique tracer vector that mixes along conservatively. When an outcome arrives, the local tracer composition is unmixed by solving a small mass-balance system, giving each past decision's exact share. Each link then scales its routing fraction by (1 + rate × outcome × credited tracer it carried), and nodes renormalize.
   DIFFERENCE: Exact credit across arbitrarily long delays in one forward pass, with no backward sweep or decaying trace.
   nearest published work: GFlowNet flow-conservation credit, RTRL / forward-mode sensitivities
