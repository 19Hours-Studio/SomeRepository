QUESTION the ledger serves: {QUESTION}

3-5 archetypes (concrete product + way it is made and delivered) most relevant to the question, each as a competent
operator runs it today (best current practice). Tools: WebSearch (WebFetch does not reach hosts here; use excerpts) and
Bash with python3. At most 15 searches. Write files only in the current directory; do not commit.

For each archetype give: best current practice in one line; delivered cost per unit of final product at the point of
sale or use; the cost split in % that sums to 100 across exactly these terms: energy | labour | inbound logistics |
outbound logistics | processing | capital | other (taxes, royalties, fees, margins no engineering can touch); the
largest term; the price.

Final answer (text only), exactly this shape, at most 700 words:
=== LEDGER ===
