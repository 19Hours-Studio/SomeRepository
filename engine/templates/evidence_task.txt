# Task: the evidence ledger - what is reliably known

QUESTION the ledger serves: {QUESTION}

You are a careful researcher. Build the ledger of HARD FACTS that any explanation must account for, with sources. Use
WebSearch (WebFetch does not resolve domains in this environment; use search-result excerpts) and python3 for any
counting or statistics. Work only in `{WORKDIR}`: read no other files in the repository, write files only there, do
not commit.

Cover everything measurable or documented: how many objects or cases exist, where and when (distribution, dating),
dimensions and their variation, materials and manufacture, every distinctive feature and how consistent it is across
examples, wear and damage, find contexts and associated objects, inscriptions or texts, related or variant objects,
what is conspicuously ABSENT (places, texts, depictions, wear), and the most recent finds and studies (2023-2026).
Separate what is measured from what is interpreted. Mark each fact HARD (measured or documented in several sources)
or SOFT (single source, disputed). Give numbers wherever they exist.

Final answer (text only), exactly this shape, at most 1,200 words in total:
=== EVIDENCE ===
FACT 1: <the fact, with numbers> | HARD/SOFT | <source URL or reference>
(25-40 facts, the most constraining first)
GAPS: 3-6 lines - what is not known that would decide between explanations, and which existing collection or record
could answer it.
