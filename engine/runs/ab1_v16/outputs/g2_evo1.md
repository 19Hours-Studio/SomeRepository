Crossover notes and honesty check:

Pair 1 (instrument channels x commutation carving). The two parents become one loop. Each operation pair whose commutation status is still undecided becomes a target for a new probe. Each new probe is in turn logged as an operation, so its own commutation with the world is tested too. If a probe doesn't commute with what it observes, it is disturbing what it measures. Closest work is active structure learning / experimental design for causal discovery and the Alpha algorithm. Mutation: here the target is commutation decidability, and measurement back-action is learned, not prediction loss.

Pair 2 (path-multiplicity x commit-reveal). Before a walk runs, it must seal a hashed forecast of its endpoint. Only walks that confirm a sealed forecast count toward the tally. Primitives that keep producing forecast departures that the evidence doesn't justify are dissolved. Closest work is self-consistency/TTRL voting and preregistration. Mutation: the forecasts are sealed against disjoint fact partitions, so support that is circular or post hoc can't mint a concept.

Pair 3 (pharmacovigilance x WLS bad-data detection). Adverse-event signals become redundant meters for per-update harm. The residual test can blame either an update or a reporter cohort, so poisoned complaints can't trigger rollbacks. Closest work is influence functions, Dawid-Skene reporter reliability and robust regression. Mutation: it is a joint estimate over updates and reporters, linked through the provenance graph, and the result directly sets the size of each subtraction.

=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Back-Action Instrument Algebra
MECHANISM: The system logs experience as operation sequences and tests adjacent swaps. When a pair's commutation can't be decided from current inputs, it designs a probe (hook, query, sensor setting) that observes the intermediate state, and attaches the probe as a typed channel. Deployed probes are themselves logged as operations. A probe that fails to commute with what it observes is marked invasive and fused with that process, while non-invasive probes stay as free parallel channels. A channel is pruned when removing it leaves every commutation verdict unchanged.
VALUE: The system builds its own perception around the world's independence structure and knows which of its measurements disturb what they measure.

IDEA 2
NAME: Sealed-Forecast Concept Minting
MECHANISM: Before each background walk derives from a random fact partition, it publishes a salted hash of its forecast endpoint to an append-only log. The walk then reveals the forecast once it finishes. A conclusion's tally counts only confirmations of forecasts that were sealed by walks drawing on disjoint partitions, and passing the threshold freezes the conclusion into a primitive. A verifier dissolves primitives whose use correlates with forecast departures beyond what the evidence justifies.
VALUE: Abstractions can grow without outside labels yet stay auditably free of self-confirming circular support.

IDEA 3
NAME: Update-Harm State Estimation
MECHANISM: Provenance links every weight update to the user cohorts whose interactions it touched. Cohort adverse-event rates (corrections, re-asks, abandonments) become redundant meters in a weighted least-squares estimate of each update's harm coefficient. The largest normalized residual above 3 is attributed either to an update, which is scaled back in proportion to its estimated harm, or to a reporter cohort, which is re-typed as suspect and down-weighted. The system then re-estimates until all residuals pass.
VALUE: A continually learning model can roll back genuinely bad lessons while staying robust to coordinated or corrupt feedback that tries to trigger false rollbacks.
