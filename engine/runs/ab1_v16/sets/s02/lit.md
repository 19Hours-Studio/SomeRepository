L01 | PARTIAL | closest work: Learning-To-Measure: In-Context Active Feature Acquisition (2025) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.12624 ; Modality Plug-and-Play: Elastic Modality Adaptation in Multimodal LLMs for Embodied AI / mPnP-LLM (2023) https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07886 (also Perception Evolution Networks, which add input channels online: https://technav.ieee.org/topic/perception-evolution-networks/) | Each piece exists separately (choosing which features to acquire, attaching or dropping modality encoders at runtime, growing input layers), but no work found where the system writes its own sensor, instrumentation or assay probe, triggered by stalled loss, and then adds and prunes the resulting typed channel.
L02 | PARTIAL | closest work: Editing Models with Task Arithmetic (2022) https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04089 (subtracting a weight delta, scaled by a coefficient, to remove a behavior) ; Unlearning Traces the Influential Training Data of Language Models (2024) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15241 (also ReSpect, ACL 2025, implicit rephrasing and frustration signals as feedback: https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1200; PRR with Evans criteria is a pharmacovigilance method: https://mdpi.com/1424-8247/14/1/4) | What appears new is the pharmacovigilance-style link from per-update provenance IDs through disproportionality statistics on real-use adverse events to proportional partial subtraction of individual updates; attribution and unlearning are known but unreliable at this granularity, and I found no work combining these pieces.
L03 | PARTIAL | closest work: Workflow mining / Alpha algorithm (2004) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_algorithm (parallelism inferred when both event orders are observed) and Mazurkiewicz Trace theory https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_theory (independence = swappable adjacent actions) ; Symmetry-Based Disentangled Representation Learning requires Interaction with Environments (2019) https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.00243 (factors = commuting action subgroups learned from interaction) | The new part is turning swap-and-compare commutation tests on the system's own experience log into an online rewrite of its architecture (always-commuting ops become stateless parallel threads, never-commuting ops are fused); the independence-from-commutation principle itself is old.
L04 | PARTIAL | closest work: Linear State Estimation and Bad Data Detection for Power Systems with RTU and PMU Measurements (2020) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.10764 (the weighted-least-squares largest normalized residual test with threshold 3 is textbook power-system bad-data identification) ; Reiter, A Theory of Diagnosis from First Principles (1987) https://www.cse.sc.edu/~mgv/csce580sp14/greinerCorrectionReiter.pdf (consistency-based diagnosis of faulty premises; de Kleer-Williams GDE/ATMS) | The algorithm is the standard LNR identify-remove-reestimate loop; the only novelty is transplanting it to logical premises and rule-applicability flags, and I found no AI work doing so, but it has no obvious residual covariance or measurement model for symbolic premises and, like critical measurements in LNR, a premise nothing else checks is undetectable.
L05 | PARTIAL | closest work: Measuring Faithfulness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning (2023) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13702 (early-answering test of whether the answer was already fixed before the reasoning) ; Utility-Aware Data Pricing: Token-Level Quality and Empirical Training Gain for LLMs (2026) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.22893 (hash commitments and hash-chained ledgers for tamper-evident model auditing; see also TensorCommitments https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12630 and the generic commit-reveal scheme https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commitment_scheme) | New is pairing a salted hash commitment of the model's pre-evidence answer distribution with a statistical justified-departure check and a training penalty, so third parties can audit belief revision; the commitment only binds emitted output, not internal belief, and the check needs a trustworthy reference update, so the hard part is untouched.
L06 | PARTIAL | closest work: DreamCoder: Growing generalizable, interpretable knowledge with wake-sleep Bayesian program learning (2020) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.08381 (abstraction sleep turns recurrent fragments into new library primitives) ; Chunking in Soar: The Anatomy of a General Learning Mechanism (1986) https://mlanthology.org/mlj/1986/laird1986mlj-chunking (also NELL, which promotes beliefs on multi-source agreement: https://papers.baulab.info/papers/knowledge/Carlson-2010.pdf; Lao, Mitchell, Cohen random-walk inference over a KB 2011: https://course.khoury.northeastern.edu/cs6200s14/ssl/paper/lao-emnlp11.pdf; TTRL, label-free agreement across sampled routes as signal: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16084) | The distinctive element is using the count of intermediate-fact-disjoint derivation routes as the label-free promotion criterion (and dissolving primitives when redundancy is lost), rather than usage frequency, compression gain or extractor agreement; note that promoted conclusions are already implied by stored facts, so they add reuse and speed rather than new information.
NOTE: Every idea assembles known components (active feature acquisition, task arithmetic, process mining and trace theory, power-system LNR, commit-reveal, library-learning chunking), and the novelty is mostly a borrowed-from-another-field transplant (pharmacovigilance PRR, power-system state estimation, concurrency theory, cryptographic commitments) plus a specific trigger or criterion, with no close combined precedent found by my searches (1-3 per idea, each idea checked mainly through standard-mode searches, so a miss is not proof of absence). L04 and L03 are the most nearly known, since their core algorithms exist verbatim in power engineering and process mining, while L01 and L02 are the least covered as a combined mechanism. Several of the ideas also lean on an unspecified hard part (a noise model for L04, trustworthy attribution for L02, an honest prior for L05, a state-equality test for L03) that the literature suggests is where such mechanisms usually fail.
