PAIR 1
 Parent A: MECHANISM: The input layer has a variable width. When the system cannot reduce its loss on some quantity from its existing inputs, it writes and deploys an external probe: a code instrumentation hook, a sensor configuration, a lab-assay protocol or a database query. Whatever stream the probe produces is attached as a new typed input channel with its own learned encoder. Channels whose removal does not hurt downstream performance are pruned, so over time the system's perceptual alphabet becomes a set of instruments it designed itself.
   VALUE: It can perceive and reason about quantities that no human ever chose to give it, instead of being limited to the fixed modalities it was trained on.
   nearest published work: Learning-To-Measure: In-Context Active Feature Acquisition (2025) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.12624 ; Modality Plug-and-Play: Elastic Modality Adaptation in Multimodal LLMs for Embodied AI / mPnP-LLM (2023) https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07886 (also Perception Evolution Networks, which add input channels online: https://technav.ieee.org/topic/perception-evolution-networks/)
 Parent B: MECHANISM: Background random walks keep re-deriving conclusions from stored facts along many different routes. A tally records how many independent routes reach each conclusion (routes sharing no intermediate fact count separately). When the tally passes a threshold, the conclusion is frozen into a new atomic primitive that later walks can use as one step. Primitives that are no longer reachable by several routes are dissolved back into their parts.
   VALUE: It builds new concepts from how well a conclusion is held together by the rest of what it knows, with no outside feedback, so abstraction can grow in domains where nothing ever says "correct."
   nearest published work: DreamCoder: Growing generalizable, interpretable knowledge with wake-sleep Bayesian program learning (2020) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.08381 (abstraction sleep turns recurrent fragments into new library primitives) ; Chunking in Soar: The Anatomy of a General Learning Mechanism (1986) https://mlanthology.org/mlj/1986/laird1986mlj-chunking (also NELL, which promotes beliefs on multi-source agreement: https://papers.baulab.info/papers/knowledge/Carlson-2010.pdf; Lao, Mitchell, Cohen random-walk inference over a KB 2011: https://course.khoury.northeastern.edu/cs6200s14/ssl/paper/lao-emnlp11.pdf; TTRL, label-free agreement across sampled routes as signal: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16084)

PAIR 2
 Parent A: MECHANISM: Each experience is logged as an ordered sequence of operations. A background process swaps neighbouring pairs and tests whether the resulting record is identical. Operations that always commute are split into separate parallel threads with no shared state. Operations that never commute are fused into one indivisible unit, so the system's architecture becomes a map of the world's independence structure.
   VALUE: The system can split the world into independent parts and recombine them freely, which gives built-in compositional generalization.
   nearest published work: Workflow mining / Alpha algorithm (2004) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_algorithm (parallelism inferred when both event orders are observed) and Mazurkiewicz Trace theory https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_theory (independence = swappable adjacent actions) ; Symmetry-Based Disentangled Representation Learning requires Interaction with Environments (2019) https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.00243 (factors = commuting action subgroups learned from interaction)
 Parent B: MECHANISM: Before the system sees evidence or produces a reasoning trace, it must publish a cryptographic hash of its quantized answer distribution, plus a salt, to an external log it cannot change. After the evidence arrives, it reveals the commitment. An independent verifier then checks whether the final answer departs from the committed one more than the evidence statistically justifies. Training penalizes departures without justification and rewards accurate commitments, so the architecture learns to keep "what I believed beforehand" separate from "what I can rationalize afterward".
   VALUE: Third parties can check that the system is not rewriting its beliefs after the fact, which is a property no current LLM can prove about itself.
   nearest published work: Measuring Faithfulness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning (2023) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13702 (early-answering test of whether the answer was already fixed before the reasoning) ; Utility-Aware Data Pricing: Token-Level Quality and Empirical Training Gain for LLMs (2026) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.22893 (hash commitments and hash-chained ledgers for tamper-evident model auditing; see also TensorCommitments https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12630 and the generic commit-reveal scheme https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commitment_scheme)
