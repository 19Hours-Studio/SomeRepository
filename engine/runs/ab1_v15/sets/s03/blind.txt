K01
MECHANISM: Every double-entry balance identity is treated as a surveying condition equation. A least-squares adjustment runs over all transfers, and Baarda w-tests compare two kinds of explanation: "a blunder in step k" (a residual pattern local to one step) and "an unseen holder h" (one systematic pattern shared across many balance loops). Hidden holders are added the way self-calibration adds parameters: only when they pass a significance test and lower the minimal detectable bias of other claims. Variance-component estimation learns a reliability weight for each quantity, which continuously demotes a quantity toward "approximately conserved". Redundancy numbers point the planner to the claims nothing checks yet, and it then asks for or derives the one extra count that would close a loop around them.
VALUE: The system can track world state over very long texts and say which step is wrong or which unmentioned participant must exist, with a stated confidence in each verdict.

K02
MECHANISM: When a behavior fails, the system creates small parameter lesions that reproduce the failure. Pairs of lesions are "crossed" by mixing activations from the two lesioned copies. If the mix restores the behavior, the lesions sit in different functional units. If it doesn't, they sit in the same one. The groups found this way define module boundaries, and parameters are re-partitioned and re-routed to match. A map of the groups is stored and updated as new failures appear.
VALUE: The module structure comes from functional evidence, not from a design chosen in advance, so fixes, transfer and growth stay local to the unit that is actually responsible.

K03
MECHANISM: Claims are nodes, and derivations are edges that carry transformations; whatever error is left after going round a loop is that loop's misclosure. When the Hodge split finds a harmonic residual, the system doesn't accept it as a real gap straight away. First it builds bridging derivations across the gap, choosing them with Baarda's minimal-detectable-bias so that each new derivation best tells the possible explanations apart. If the new pieces bring the error back to zero, it was a mistake, and the w-test finds the bad step. If the error survives every bridge, the system creates a new context variable that splits the claims into separate frames.
VALUE: The system can tell a mistake in its own reasoning apart from a real need for separate frames, and it finds out by running its own test.

K04
MECHANISM: Holders are learned points, and each transfer is a bar between two of them. A balanced set of transfers is an equilibrium stress, so a quantity counts as conserved only while its transfers stay in that stress space. An imbalance that won't go away is treated as a load on the framework. The system adds a hidden holder at the position where the loose directions (floppy modes) can absorb that load. Where stresses conflict, the holder splits in two.
VALUE: The system can keep exact track of world state, and the geometry shows where unknown participants must be and which quantities are not really conserved.

K05
MECHANISM: A new fact first goes into a queue. At each stage it is checked against a different, separate subset of the roughly 64 ensemble members, and only after other facts have been absorbed in between. Agreement between those members gives the fact's binding margin, and a fact rejected at any stage is gone for good. The number of stages grows with how much of the model the update would change. Facts that pass more stages get smaller observation noise, so the update counts them more.
VALUE: The system can keep learning from a stream of new facts, and wrong facts that would cause broad damage are stopped before they get in.

K06
MECHANISM: For pairs of cues that compete over an internal feature, the system measures dose-response shifts, meaning how much more of cue A is needed as cue B increases, and fits a Schild slope. A slope of 1 confirms that one mechanism mediates both cues. If the slope differs from 1, the feature is split into two sites, each with its own learned gate. The slopes are stored per feature, and they are re-tested whenever new competing cues show up.
VALUE: Features that are secretly several mechanisms get found and separated by a quantitative invariant, so the model's internals become more faithful and reasoning that combines cues becomes more reliable.

K07
MECHANISM: The system continuously runs the four-point condition on embedding distances among concepts in its taxonomy. Quartets that violate it point to hybrid concepts, and those concepts are re-typed as reticulation nodes with two weighted parent lineages. Properties are then inherited from both parents. The violation magnitudes are stored, and edges are reweighted as evidence accumulates.
VALUE: The system can represent and reason about concepts that blend lineages, such as loanwords, hybrid organisms or mixed genres, which strictly tree-shaped hierarchies get wrong.

K08
MECHANISM: Each proofreading stage re-encodes the context with a learned map (paraphrase, abstraction, dropout). A step's binding margins across these encodings therefore form edge flows on a cycle complex, and a Hodge decomposition splits them into three parts. The gradient part is encoder bias: correct the map and keep the step. The curl part is real fragility: discard the step irreversibly. The harmonic part means the step holds only in some contexts: commit it locally to a new chart, with an explicit context variable and a transition map. Later steps that cross chart boundaries face extra proofreading stages, scaled to the misclosure of the transition map they cross. A new chart is kept only if steps committed in it survive this proofreading.
VALUE: Error rates in long reasoning drop exponentially without discarding conclusions that are true only in some contexts, and the system learns where its knowledge splits into separate frames.
