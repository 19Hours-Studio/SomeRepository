Three directions follow. Each one fits a single static page with Russian copy and a placeholder catalogue (ПОР-001…), and each puts the newest release, artists, news, the contact addresses and the demo policy one tap from the top navigation. None uses a literal door picture. «Порог» shapes the structure instead: a stair profile, the dead-wax zone of a record, or a gate threshold in audio.

=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Ступени каталога
MECHANISM: The central move is a stair profile. Each section's top edge steps down one riser from the one before it, and the steps follow the catalogue order, so the page is literally a descent through the label's releases. From the top, the page holds: a sticky plain-text nav (Релизы, Артисты, Новости, О лейбле, Демо, Контакты); the label statement next to the newest release, ПОР-012LP, with a CSS-drawn cover, a play button that sounds a short Web Audio placeholder tone, and links to listen or buy; the full catalogue as a ledger table with a row per release that expands to show its tracklist and credits; three artist profiles with bio, roles and a booking address; four dated news posts; the demo rules; and a footer with separate inboxes for demos, press and booking. The palette is limestone grey and dark oak with one brass accent for the sill line and focus rings, in light and dark token sets. Type is Unbounded for display at three sizes only, Golos Text for body, and IBM Plex Mono for catalogue data. The detail only a label has is the format suffix and run line on each step, for example "ПОР-009MC · кассета · тираж 94", "ПОР-010LP · 180 г · 300 экз." and "℗© Порог, 2026".
VALUE: A visitor can hear, read about and contact the label on one page, and will remember walking down the catalogue step by step.

IDEA 2
NAME: Мёртвая дорожка
MECHANISM: The central move uses the dead wax, the bare ring between the last groove and the centre label that is a record's own threshold. Each release card reproduces its run-out etching as real information: the matrix number, side, test-pressing status and pressing date. From the top, the page holds: a header split into «Сторона А» for listeners and «Сторона Б» for artists and press, with both anchor sets always visible; Side A with the newest release, an SVG vinyl drawn in concentric grooves with tap-to-play placeholder audio and listen or buy links, then the release grid, artists and news; Side B with the demo rules, an EPK block (bio, discography, downloadable-looking but text-only press facts, embargo note) and the split contact addresses. The palette is oxblood centre-label paper on pale vinyl grey with off-white text panels. Type is Tenor Sans for display and PT Sans with PT Mono for etched data. Motion is limited to a slow groove rotation that stops under reduced motion. The detail only a record has is the etched line on every card, for example "ПОР-007-A1 · ТП одобрен 14.08 · прессовка 4–6 нед."
VALUE: Listeners get music first, while artists and press get their own clearly labelled half. People remember it as the site where every release shows its matrix like a real pressing.

IDEA 3
NAME: Порог срабатывания
MECHANISM: The central move takes «порог» as the threshold of a noise gate. One red horizontal line runs at the same height through every release waveform on the page, and only the sound above it "opens" the gate. From the top, the page holds: the nav and label statement; the newest release with a large SVG waveform, the threshold line, a placeholder play button that lights the peaks above the line as a tone plays, and listen or buy links; a release list with a small waveform, date, catalogue number, format and an expandable tracklist and credits for each; artist profiles; news; a demo section titled «Как пройти порог» (demos accepted, 1–3 finished tracks, private link only, genre and links in the email, reply in about two weeks, a local form that confirms on the page); and the split contacts. The palette is cool paper white and graphite with signal-blue waveforms and one red threshold line, mirrored for dark mode. Type is Commissioner for display and body with IBM Plex Mono for dB and catalogue data. The detail only a sound label has is that each release states its gate setting and loudness as liner data, for example "порог −42 dBFS · −14 LUFS", next to the ℗© line.
VALUE: Visitors find releases, artists and the demo rules in seconds, and they remember a demo policy literally framed as passing a threshold.
