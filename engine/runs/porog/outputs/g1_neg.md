**The 8 usual builds**
1. A full-bleed hero photo, a "Latest release" button and a social-icon footer (a Squarespace or Wix template).
2. Black and white with a wall of square covers.
3. A hero carousel of new releases, with rounded cards below.
4. A single Bandcamp or Spotify embed doing the work of a player.
5. A literal door or doorway silhouette as the logo and hero image.
6. A dark "moody" page with one neon accent and a hamburger menu.
7. Centered sections stacked as Hero → Releases → Artists → News → Contact, each in the same card.
8. One `contact@` address plus "send us your demo".

**The shared paradigm:** a generic brochure in which the label's name is decoration and its catalogue is a gallery of pictures rather than a body of information.

The three concepts below make the label's own working materials the structure of the page: its catalogue numbers, the record as an object, and the sound threshold.

=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Каталог ПОР как оглавление
MECHANISM: The page is organised as the label's catalogue ledger, and the catalogue number is the navigation. Every release is ПОР-001, ПОР-002 and so on, the prefix being the first three letters of «Порог», with a format suffix (ПОР-007LP, ПОР-008MC), and a sticky index rail lists these numbers next to the sections Новое, Релизы, Артисты, Новости, Демо, Контакты. From top to bottom:
- a two-line statement of the label;
- the newest release, open in full, with an SVG cover built from nested rectangular frames, a tracklist with durations, the date, the format and run ("180 г, 300 экз., пронумерованы"), credits, the ℗© line, a placeholder player marked "образец", and plain links to listen or buy;
- the older releases as collapsible ledger rows (`<details>`);
- artist profiles cross-linked to their catalogue numbers;
- dated news;
- demo rules: the label accepts unsolicited demos, wants one to three tracks as a private link, and replies within two weeks;
- separate inboxes for demos, press and booking, shown as selectable text.

The palette is index-card stock grey-blue with ink-navy text and one rubber-stamp maroon used only on catalogue numbers. Unbounded is the display face, set only for numbers and headings, with Golos Text for body copy and JetBrains Mono for the ledger. The detail only this subject would have is the "ПОР-" numbering that encodes release order and format.
VALUE: A listener, an artist or a journalist reaches any release, rule or address within seconds from one index, and remembers a label whose catalogue literally spells its name.

IDEA 2
NAME: Сторона А, сторона Б
MECHANISM: The page is built as a record with two sides for two audiences. A sticky switch reading «А · слушателям / Б · артистам и прессе» jumps between them, and both sides remain in the page and readable without JavaScript.
- **Side A** holds the statement, the latest releases with tracklists, artists and news. Each side heading is timed like a pressing ("Сторона А — 19:40"), and that time is the true sum of the listed tracks.
- **Between the sides** a band styled as a run-out groove carries a matrix etching ("ПОР-004-A ℗© Порог, 2026") and the main contact email.
- **Side B** holds the demo rules, a press kit block (bio, discography, an embargo note), booking contacts per artist, and a demo form handled on the page.

The palette takes centre-label paper ochre, groove graphite and off-white. Covers are drawn as concentric SVG rings that open into a gap. Russo One sets the side letters only, and Golos Text sets everything else. A slow spin on the run-out band is turned off under prefers-reduced-motion. The detail only this subject would have is the side timings and the run-out matrix etching.
VALUE: Each visitor picks their side in one tap and finds music or submission terms at once, and they remember the label as a record they "flipped."

IDEA 3
NAME: Порог срабатывания
MECHANISM: The page takes "threshold" in its sound-engineering sense, the line a noise gate opens at, and uses one horizontal threshold line as its visual system. A fixed dB ruler at the left edge acts as navigation, with marks for the sections Новое, Релизы, Артисты, Новости, Демо, Контакты.
- **Each release** gets an SVG waveform generated from its track durations, and only the peaks above the threshold line are coloured. Alongside it sit the tracklist, catalogue number, date, credits, ℗© line and listen links.
- **The home block** puts the label statement at the line, with a placeholder player clearly marked as such.
- **The demo section** is titled «Как пройти порог» and states the terms plainly: a private link, one to three finished tracks, a reply within two weeks.
- **Contacts** are split by purpose.

The palette is concrete pale grey with graphite text and a single signal amber, the colour of a gate-open LED. Unbounded sets the headings and IBM Plex Mono sets the dB values. The only motion is a short gate-flash when a heading enters view, and there is none under reduced motion. The detail only this subject would have is that the line on screen is a real audio-engineering term that matches the label's name.
VALUE: Visitors scan releases and demo terms quickly along one line, and they remember a label whose name is also a studio control.
