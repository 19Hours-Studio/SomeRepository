W01
MECHANISM: In the background, the system copies verified source values (numbers, relations, entities) into its belief store and corrupts them by graded known amounts. It then measures what fraction of each corruption its next inferences pull back toward what neighbouring beliefs imply. This restoring-force slope is stored for each region of the belief store. A slope near zero marks the region as uncheckable. A real belief that deviates from its neighbour-implied value by more than the measured slope allows is flagged as a likely blunder and revised. Regions whose slope drops trigger targeted relearning, and the slope is measured again afterwards. First test: Load Wikidata country statistics, which are linked by identities such as GDP/population, plant graded corruptions, and fit per-region restoring slopes; success means slope-thresholded flagging catches separately planted unknown blunders with precision > 0.8 in high-slope regions and near chance in low-slope regions.
VALUE: Without an oracle or self-grading, the system can tell which of its beliefs are actually held in check by other knowledge, and can find specific errors there using only errors it planted with known size.

W02
MECHANISM: For each stored belief, the system runs a cheap twin forward pass with that belief masked out. Whenever a new raw observation arrives (a document token, sensor value, tool output or user reply), it records the difference in prediction error between the full pass and the masked pass. This running per-belief credit is the only quality signal. It sets how strongly each belief is weighted and how much new data may overwrite it. Beliefs with persistently negative credit are retracted, and sources whose added beliefs earn negative credit are down-weighted. First test: Inject 1,000 CounterFact beliefs (half true, half false) as maskable prefixes into Pythia-1B and stream subsequent Wikipedia text; success means accumulated masked-vs-full prediction-error credit separates true from false beliefs with AUROC > 0.75.
VALUE: Every belief is graded continuously by the outside world's next data rather than by the model's own judgment, so learning can stay open-ended in domains with no labels.

W03
MECHANISM: For each claim, the system makes two small weight edits: one that asserts the claim and one that asserts its negation. It then measures how well each edited model predicts a held-out stream of raw data (documents, logs, sensor records) that was not used for the edit. The difference in log-likelihood, compared against a null distribution from edits of random claims, is stored as the claim's support. Claims the world prefers are consolidated, and claims whose negation predicts better are retracted. Claims that sit inside the null band are labelled "uncontrolled by any data" rather than trusted. First test: Apply ROME assert/negate edits to GPT-2 XL for 300 CounterFact claims and score held-out Wikipedia paragraphs about each subject against a random-claim null; success means the sign matches truth for more than 75% of claims, and null-band claims are mostly those whose subjects lack held-out text.
VALUE: Unlabeled raw data, not the model's opinion, decides whether each belief is right, wrong or unverifiable.

W04
MECHANISM: Before every retrieval, calculator call, database query or sensor read made during ordinary work, the system hash-commits a probability distribution over the result. The returned value settles the wager immediately. Parametric weights are trained only on these settled log-scores, never on answers the system grades itself. Log-scores are also stored per topic and tool, giving a running, tamper-proof record of where its memory can be relied on. First test: Have Llama-3-8B commit distributions before 50k logged calculator and Wikidata lookups, then fine-tune on the settled log-scores; success means held-out tool-result log-loss drops at least 10% versus a self-graded fine-tune, and per-topic log-scores predict closed-book accuracy (Spearman > 0.6).
VALUE: Every action the system takes in the world becomes a correctness signal it cannot fake, so it gets millions of uncontaminated grades a day instead of waiting months for the future to arrive.

W05
MECHANISM: One model answers each question at a shallow and at a deep compute budget. Each run also predicts the distribution of answers the other run will give. An answer is credited when it is more common than the cross-predictions expected, and deep chains are rewarded when they correctly predict the shallow answers while the shallow runs fail to predict them. This Bayesian-Truth-Serum score is the reward for revising weights. The system stores per-domain asymmetry statistics and stops training wherever the asymmetry disappears. First test: Run Qwen2.5-7B on 1,000 MATH problems at 64- and 1024-token budgets, with cross-predicted answer distributions, and no training; success means the BTS score's AUROC for ground-truth correctness beats self-consistency majority-vote confidence by at least 0.05.
VALUE: It gives a correctness reward without an oracle, based on an information asymmetry that the model cannot fake by agreeing with itself.
