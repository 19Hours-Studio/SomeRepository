NOTE: Most answers that take on the crux use one of three moves. The redundancy-based ones all quietly assume their error sources are independent, so errors shared across sources slip through. Many entries are transplants from other fields that recur in near-duplicate variants (F19/F28/F33/F44, F12/F36/F43, F24/F27), and the few that fall in the listed families are re-descriptions of known methods.
