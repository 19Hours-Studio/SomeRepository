FAMILY | LEDGER | Persistent cross-run ledger of every family found at any layer, all forbidden from the start of the next run, plus replicated, shuffled judge scoring ranked by lower confidence bound to filter out noise | M01
FAMILY | FORECAST | A forecaster agent predicts the likely outputs or families in advance; originality is computed as distance from those forecasts and from a cross-run archive, with paired replicates and bandit technique selection gated on variance | M02, M09
FAMILY | PROBANCH | Literature or retrieval moved to the front to build evidence-backed problem cards or constraint dossiers; every idea must name the documented cost or constraint it removes, and value is scored against that evidence; unsolved items persist and gain weight across runs | M03, M07
FAMILY | KILLTEST | In-pipeline falsification: each survivor carries cheap, pre-registered decisive checks (Fermi estimate, code simulation, search with a predicted result) that a verifier or red-team agent runs; value is weighted by survival, and failure patterns are stored to steer later runs and crossover | M04, M06, M08
FAMILY | GRAFT | Asymmetric crossover: the more concrete parent becomes a fixed build sheet, the other parent grafts exactly one mechanism into a named step, a checker rejects blurred fields, and successful pairings are remembered | M05
