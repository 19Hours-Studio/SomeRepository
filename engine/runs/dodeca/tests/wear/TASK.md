each with a round hole, knobs at the 20 corners, north-western provinces, 2nd-4th c. AD) predict different traces:
- a divination LOT thrown onto cloth or a table: all 20 knob tips evenly rounded beyond their bases; hole rims and
  interior clean;
- a STAFF MOUNT or emblem on a rod: abrasion, organic or mineral-preserved wood/bone inside the larger unringed

- a CANDLE SHADE: soot and fat along one opposite pair of holes;
