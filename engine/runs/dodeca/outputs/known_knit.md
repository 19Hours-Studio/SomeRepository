Control: the glove-finger knitting explanation as proposed (Martin Hallett, early 2020s; attempts.md ATTEMPT 10), written in its strongest form.

=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Glove-finger knitting frame
MECHANISM: It was a knitting frame of the "knitting nancy" kind: yarn was looped round the five knobs that frame one face and lifted over them row by row, so a knitted tube grew down through that face's hole into the hollow body and out through the opposite hole. Each hole gives a tube of a different width, for fingers of different sizes, which is why the holes differ within every piece and between pieces; the knobs are the pegs, and the hollow body guides the tube. Gloves were needed in the cold north-western provinces, which is why the objects are found there and not around the Mediterranean.
VALUE: It gives a working role to the knobs, the hollow body and the varied holes together, and links the distribution to climate. Polish on the knobs of the faces used, or textile fibre traces, would support it; knob surfaces as fresh as the recesses on pieces with original surface refute it.
