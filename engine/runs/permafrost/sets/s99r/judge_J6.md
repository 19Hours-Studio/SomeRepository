TOP3:
R44: it goes straight at the separation crux. The ore powers its own separator, so no power supply is brought in. It is the most buildable spec, and one bench test settles it: does each stage send forward 85% of the spodumene and only 45% of the waste?
R40: freezing the leach liquor does two jobs at once. It crystallises copper sulphate, and the heat it releases runs an engine that powers electrowinning. The heat-rejection and engine-output numbers hold to about 1.3x, and it hits the crux directly.
R28: the most original single move in the set. The metal travels as a gas inside an existing trunk gas line. The physics holds, so it is worth a costed hazard study, even though the whole chain gains only about 1-2x and nickel carbonyl is highly toxic.
NOTE: The set falls into a few basins: 8 ICEHULL, 7 FRZCONC, 5 under-ice pods, and 3 each of carbonyl, electrostatic, freeze-engine, sublimation, boulder-lift and ice-dam. Every self-audited "anchor kept" answer shrinks its claim to a niche of 0.5-2.3x, while the short unaudited answers keep "several times cheaper" claims that fail quick checks (pykrete buoyancy and melting, freeze-thaw cracking rates, ice-versus-rail friction). Only the electrostatic, freeze-engine, sublimation and eutectic families attack the separation crux, and none of them beats 2x.
