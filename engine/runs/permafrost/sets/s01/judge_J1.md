TOP3:
- A13: Frozen placer gravel is treated as a dry, brittle feed. That takes out water, thawing and tailings ponds, so it goes straight at the separation crux, and one winter of trials would show whether it works.
- A07: The near-zero moisture of −40 °C air helps triboelectric separation, which needs very dry grains to keep their charge. That makes dry concentration on site possible with almost no power, though fine-grain liberation is still unproven.
- A05: Pods carried by the current under river ice cost almost nothing to move. A few instrumented sacks under one river's ice would test the idea, although frazil ice and hanging dams could kill it.
