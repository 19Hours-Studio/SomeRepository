READING | Year-round bulk haulage to market | A way to move millions of tonnes a year of ore, coal or concentrate from roadless deposits to a railhead or sea port, every year, at a fraction of today's cost per tonne-km. | CRUX: Haulage that keeps going in any season and any weather at real tonnage; the typical answers rely on one spring flood a year, winter-only ice or the right wind, and leave hulls surviving ice jams, steering, return logistics and transfer at the Arctic estuary unsolved.
READING | Ship value, not rock | A way to turn raw ore into a saleable high-grade product at the deposit, so only a few percent of the mass ever has to be moved. | CRUX: Separating and recovering the metal in deep cold with almost no power or reagents brought in; the typical answers use free frost to crack rock or concentrate brine, but assume that mineral separation, slow leaching near freezing, reagent supply and site power will simply work.
READING | Monetise stranded oil and gas | A way to bring Siberian and Arctic gas and oil, where most of the value lies, to market or into use without new pipelines or LNG megaprojects. | CRUX: Cheaply turning hydrocarbons at small, scattered wellheads into something that can be shipped or used on site; the typical answers deal only with solid ores, treat gas as a free heat source and never move hydrocarbons at all.
