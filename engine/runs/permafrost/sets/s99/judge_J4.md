TOP3:
Z39 - Truly new route: the gas line carries the nickel, separation happens by the Mond process, and the numbers have been checked (0.04 mol% stays below condensation, the gas carries the metal at about 150x cheaper than truck and rail). Toxicity is the real test.
Z38 (with the Z20 repair) - Attacks the separation crux head-on: the grains' own triboelectric charge drives the separating field, so no power supply, water or reagents are needed. A cold, dry-air test rig is cheap to build.
Z32 - Sorts placer gravel in place by sublimation in wind and spring sun, with no water, pumps or reagents. The rates are checked and give about 3.6x lower cost, which a single test pad could confirm or refute in one spring.
NOTE: About 40% of the set falls into just two basins, pykrete flood barges (8) and freeze concentration (8), and nearly all transport ideas leave the separation crux untouched.
