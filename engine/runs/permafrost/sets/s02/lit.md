Existing work finds that heating the feed above about 80 °C helps, and that very low humidity is not automatically better. Gold and diamond ore are not typical triboelectric targets.

NOTE: Four of the six ideas (L03, L04, L05, L06) rest on components that are individually old or natural (tribo-electrostatic separation, solar thaw of stripped muck, Transpolar Drift ice-rafting, dry washers). The novelty is in the Arctic-specific recombination, so they are PARTIAL rather than NO-CLOSE-MATCH, and none of these combinations turned up in a costed study. L01 and L02 are the only ones with no close match, but they are one idea in two forms, and the nearest literature is generic capsule hydraulics and natural under-ice sediment transport, not freight under river ice. Search engines returned little Arctic-specific or Russian-language work on any idea, so absence of hits is weak evidence, and the feasibility questions (under-ice velocity and roughness, charge behaviour in cold feed, frozen-gravel disaggregation, ice survival to Fram Strait) are not answered by the literature I found.
