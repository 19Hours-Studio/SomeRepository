NOTE: Most ideas recombine established unit operations (freeze concentration, tribo-electrostatics, capsule transport, thaw-by-water, glaciothermal power) with a cold-climate twist, so the novelty is in the specific coupling and siting rather than in any single physical effect. The under-ice river lane family (W03, W06, W09) and the ice-lifted stone removal in W10 had no engineered precedent in my searches, whereas W02 (carbonyl by pipeline patents), W08 (glaciothermal power plus cryogenic copper leaching) and W05 (water erosion of frozen gravel) sit closest to published or patented work. Web search only, patent and paper dates are partly approximate, and none of the cost or feasibility numbers in the ideas were checked.
