=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Jacked Frozen-Block Stripping
MECHANISM: Overlapping hot-water-drilled holes cut frozen overburden into 20×20 m blocks, and summer creek water injected at bedrock thaws only the gold-bearing gravel beneath one block. Hydraulic jacks bracing against the next frozen block push the ~8,000-t block into the previously mined cell, exposing the thawed paystreak for excavator and sluice. Blocks advance downstream like sliding-puzzle tiles; kerf spacing adapts to measured ice content.
VALUE: Overburden is pushed rather than ripped, using about 0.1 l diesel/m³ instead of ~1 l/m³, cutting placer earthmoving roughly tenfold.

IDEA 2
NAME: Bell-Hole Thaw-Lift Mining
MECHANISM: A tracked sonic rig drills cased 0.3 m holes on a 6 m grid through frozen overburden. At bedrock a rotating hot-water jet thaws a 3 m-radius bell of paystreak, an airlift raises the slurry to a sluice, and tailings return to backfill the bell and refreeze, while frozen roof and pillars stand. Jetting time adapts to the gold grade of the returning slurry.
VALUE: Under 1% of overburden is disturbed, cutting fuel from ~11 to ~2 l diesel/g and opening placers too deep to strip.

IDEA 3
NAME: Village Coal-Slurry Wells
MECHANISM: Where coal-bearing strata lie beneath or beside a settlement, boreholes reach the seam, a warm-water jet cuts the frozen coal and an airlift lifts the slurry. The slurry is milled and thickened into coal-water fuel and pumped a few hundred metres into a retrofitted boiler, so the coal is never dried, barged, stockpiled or trucked. Wells are added as cavities exhaust.
VALUE: For settlements on coal-bearing ground, it removes the barge and winter-road haulage that makes up ~85% of boiler-gate coal cost.

IDEA 4
NAME: Snow-Bedded Seasonal Crude Hose
MECHANISM: Each November a reel-sled lays a 300-km polyurethane lay-flat hose along the winter-road route and a groomer buries it in compacted snow, which supports and insulates it; associated-gas-fired booster pumps keep light crude moving. The field produces its whole annual volume in the five winter months and is shut in over summer, so the reservoir itself is the storage tank. In April the hose is purged with nitrogen, reeled and reused.
VALUE: Replaces ~$10/bbl trucking with roughly $2-3/bbl for small stranded East-Siberian fields, with no permanent pipeline.

IDEA 5
NAME: Flooded-Pit Floating Mine
MECHANISM: After a starter cut the porphyry pit is flooded, and under winter ice its water stays a few degrees above freezing. Benches are drilled and blasted from barges or the ice, a cutter-suction dredge pumps broken ore to a concentrator built from barge modules floating on the same lake, and tailings settle into mined-out depths behind the dredge. Dredging depth and blast pattern follow the block model as the lake deepens.
VALUE: Eliminates the haul fleet, roads, crushers, dewatering, tailings dam and heated foundations, roughly halving capital, the largest cost of Arctic copper.
