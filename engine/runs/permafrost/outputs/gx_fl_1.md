=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Robot drift-mining under frozen roof
MECHANISM: Instead of stripping 5-15 m of frozen overburden, an autonomous excavator tunnels only the 1-2 m pay gravel on bedrock, using the frozen ground above as roof. Its sodium-ion pack (CAP 9) works at −40°C and emits no exhaust heat, so the roof stays frozen. Its dry-stone-walling autonomy (CAP 20) stacks cobbles into mortarless pillars behind the face and re-plans spans from face scans, so no pay is left in pillars.
VALUE: Moves 5-10x less ground per gram, attacking earthmoving, about two-thirds of physical placer cost.

IDEA 2
NAME: Woven pressure-shell concentrator
MECHANISM: Flotation cells, tanks, thickener walls and the building envelope are Vectran-strap woven shells (CAP 21) holding slurry instead of cabin air: 6 m of slurry is 0.8 bar, about a sixth of demonstrated burst. They ship folded and sit on insulated gravel pads, needing no piles, structural steel or welders; only rotors and pumps are rigid, and cells are added as throughput grows.
VALUE: Turns the steel, piling and erection core of a 41% capital share into a one-summer flat-pack build at about a tenth of the cost.

IDEA 3
NAME: Floating bluff coal miner
MECHANISM: Many river settlements sit near bluffs where coal seams outcrop. A self-propelled barge with an autonomous excavator works the bluff nearest each settlement during the navigation season, cutting frozen coal into its hold, unloading at the riverside yard, then moving to the next exposure. Its dry-stone-walling autonomy (CAP 20) re-stacks the removed rock slabs as a mortarless revetment, so the cut bank cannot thaw and slump.
VALUE: Replaces a 1,000-2,000 km barge-plus-winter-road chain with a 10-100 km haul, cutting settlement coal's 85% logistics share about tenfold.

IDEA 4
NAME: Flash-and-electrolyse lithium containers
MECHANISM: One-second Joule pulses (CAP 15), designed for rare-earth fly ash, flip carbon-mixed spodumene concentrate into an acid-leachable phase without a kiln. An ambient-temperature electrolyser (CAP 16), designed for cement, splits recycled sodium sulphate into sulphuric acid that leaches the lithium and caustic that purifies it into hydroxide, regenerating the sulphate. With no acid plant or bulk reagent imports, containers supplied by winter road replace the converter.
VALUE: Removes most of a 91 bn rub plant and the 40 bn rub all-season road, cutting a 42% capital share several- to tenfold.

IDEA 5
NAME: Superconducting rotating-field grinding pipe
MECHANISM: Polyphase rotating-field coils (CAP 11), built for truck charging, are wound with REBCO tape (CAP 6), built for fusion, around a stationary non-magnetic pipe. The field whirls loose steel needles through the ore slurry so they collide and grind it; field speed is tuned to the measured product size, and capacity grows by adding pipe sections. There is no rotating shell, girth gear, liner or 1,000-t foundation.
VALUE: Makes grinding, the heaviest and most foundation-hungry block of a porphyry concentrator's capital, roughly ten times lighter to build on permafrost.
