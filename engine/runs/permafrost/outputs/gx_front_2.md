Five answers. Each uses demonstrated capabilities in roles their inventors didn't intend and avoids the forbidden families.

=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Ore stream as heat battery
MECHANISM: Crushed gold-quartz ore waits weeks in insulated silos, charged with cheap surplus electricity to ~600 °C like the soapstone heat battery (CAP 10), then discharged through air loops to heat camp and plant through winter. Each cycle drives quartz through its 573 °C inversion, whose sudden volume jump microcracks grain boundaries, so the cooled ore grinds far more easily and releases its gold. Charging is scheduled by heat demand and power price.
VALUE: One kilowatt-hour pays twice, covering an Arctic mine's two largest energy bills: heating and grinding.

IDEA 2
NAME: Grinding mill brews its lixiviant
MECHANISM: Copper-nickel ore is dry-ground in steel-ball mills under nitrogen-hydrogen gas, so freshly abraded iron from balls and liners fixes ammonia at ~45 °C and 1 bar (CAP 14). The ammonia-rich off-gas feeds an ammoniacal leach that dissolves metals from the just-ground ore as ammine complexes; ammonia is stripped, recycled and topped up by the mill. Hydrogen comes from on-site electrolysis, and the gas mix is tuned to ore sulphur content.
VALUE: The plant runs on power, air and water alone, ending inbound reagent convoys.

IDEA 3
NAME: Resonant kimberlite unmaking
MECHANISM: Kimberlite lumps cross a water bath swept by burst-wave ultrasound of the kind that grinds kidney stones through skin (CAP 23), its frequency stepped up as fragments shrink. Stress amplitude exceeds altered kimberlite's tensile strength but stays about a hundred times below diamond's, so the matrix turns to sand while diamonds pass unbroken. Sand is screened off, leaving a small heavy residue for sorting.
VALUE: A containerised, crusher-free plant processes kimberlite and recovers large stones whole.

IDEA 4
NAME: Flash-unlocked refractory gold
MECHANISM: Arsenopyrite concentrate, conductive enough to need no carbon additive, receives one-second current pulses to ~3,000 °C (CAP 15) that destroy the sulphide lattice locking invisible gold and drive arsenic into a cold trap. The porous calcine is leached in dilute chloride, and a whey-protein amyloid aerogel (CAP 18) adsorbs the gold selectively; the loaded sponge is burned to doré. Pulse energy is tuned per batch to measured sulphur and arsenic.
VALUE: Stranded refractory gold deposits become treatable by a flown-in, cyanide-free container instead of a pressure-oxidation plant.

IDEA 5
NAME: Supply drones survey while flying
MECHANISM: Cargo drones on 500 Wh/kg cells (CAP 8) resupply remote camps, navigating by quantum magnetometers against crustal-field maps (CAP 4) where satellite navigation is jammed. The map-mismatch residuals that navigation normally discards are logged with inertial positions and fused into a steadily sharpening anomaly map. Residual patterns resembling kimberlite pipes or magnetite skarns automatically route the next flight lower over them.
VALUE: Exploration of roadless Siberia becomes a free by-product of routine logistics.
