== Round 2: iron/acid ceiling. Fe3+ sulfate hydrates and acid limit the concentration factor ==
A PLS with Fe0=2 g/L, acid 4 g/L: at 2.5 g/L Cu final Fe = 2/phi ... too high -> Fe co-crystallises.
Repair: (a) oxide-ore heap (malachite/brochantite) PLS with Cu/Fe >= 2, (b) acid-limited: run heap at low acid,
(c) stop freezing at Fe ceiling and bleed brine back to heap; recover only Cu above saturation in the bleed-limited liquor.
