=== R2: trim precision achievable in the launch slot ===
density scatter of packed snow +-10% x 1 m3 x 200 kg/m3 = +-20 kg; slot trim (waterplane 1 m2 -> 1 mm per kg) gets +-3 kg
fin pod at U>=0.2 m/s tolerates B<=~10 kg even at mu=0.5 -> trim to +3..+8 kg keeps it sliding

=== R2: freeze-in when stopped; melt in transit ===

  section mean 0.2 m/s (pod sees 0.16): drag 169 N -> trim B < 17 kg (dive angle >45 deg); trim by crane load cell holding the pod submerged (+-3 kg on ~130 N) -> OK
