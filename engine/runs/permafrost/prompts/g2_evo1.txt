If a pressure sensor detects a stall, a valve lets out gas so the sack drops back into the flow, then refills it from a cartridge. A net under a slot cut in the ice collects the sacks far downstream.
   VALUE: Freight moves along frozen rivers through the whole winter at almost no energy cost, when today nothing moves on them.

Weekly current profiles taken through the ice show where the flow stays fast. Where it slows, or where slush ice builds a dam below the ice, a relay slot is cut, the pods are lifted past the blockage and released again, and the spacing between releases is adjusted to the measured speed.
   VALUE: Ore reaches Arctic ports during the frozen months and is already on the quay, instead of competing for the short summer window on the river.
