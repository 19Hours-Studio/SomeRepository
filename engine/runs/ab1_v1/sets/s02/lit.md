NOTE: Most ideas are new combinations of established parts: reversible networks plus backward verification (L01, L04, which are close to duplicates of each other), Hodge or sheaf inconsistency detection (L02), emergent-communication or iterated-learning pressure (L03), and CRDT agent memory (L06). Only L05 has no direct precedent for its core machinery. The searches were shallow, about 2 per idea, so a miss is not proof of novelty; neural sheaf diffusion and Bennett-style reversible computing in particular were not directly checked. L01 and L04 should be treated as one idea for novelty counts.
