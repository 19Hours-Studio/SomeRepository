**Offspring 1 (Pair 1: survey adjustment × Hodge curl splitting).** Fitting each reasoning loop by least squares already gives a Hodge decomposition: the fitted part is the gradient and the misclosure is curl. So putting the two parents side by side adds nothing. In the fused version, Baarda statistics decide what the curl means. After adjustment, the flagged step is re-derived by an independent route. If the w-test residual moves to a single step and disappears on re-derivation, it was a blunder. If it stays spread evenly around a cycle, survives re-derivation, and is larger than the minimal detectable bias of any single step, it is treated as a concept ambiguity. The concept on that cycle is then split along a new latent context dimension. In return, the split changes the adjustment. The new concept copies are new network nodes with no redundancy yet, so the planner must design routes that separate them before either copy can be used.
Closest work: Baarda data snooping, HodgeRank, and temporal-consistency error finding. None of them sorts residuals into "mistake" versus "two meanings", and none plans redundancy for concepts it has just split.

**Offspring 2 (Pair 2: residual register × backward premise reconstruction).** This keeps an information balance sheet. The forward pass writes unreversible bits to the residual register (content added). The backward pass from the answer plus the premises measures the reconstruction deficit (content assumed). Each side drives the other. Residual spans are decoded into candidate premise sentences and injected into the backward pass. A candidate is promoted to a stated assumption only if it closes a matching part of the reconstruction deficit. Residual that closes no deficit is labelled fabricated. Deficit left with no residual to explain it flags a hole the model cannot name, and that triggers retrieval or a question to the user. Training minimises the bits left unmatched on both sides.
Closest work: Self-Verification, Reverse Thinking, i-RevNet, and implicit-premise generation. Those generate assumptions heuristically or check answers by plausibility. This one matches residual bits against deficit bits mechanically and turns them into explicit, testable premises.

=== IDEAS ===
IDEA 1
NAME: Blunder-or-Ambiguity Loop Adjuster
MECHANISM: Reasoning steps form redundant closed loops, adjusted by least squares. Baarda's w-test plus re-derivation sorts each misclosure: if it moves to one step and disappears on re-derivation, that step is a blunder and is fixed; if it stays spread evenly around the cycle, larger than any single step's minimal detectable bias, it is curl, and the concept is split along a new latent context dimension. Each split adds nodes with no redundancy yet, so the planner must build new independent routes that tell the two meanings apart before either can be used. The split concepts are stored with their triggering cycle and their reliability numbers.
VALUE: Long reasoning chains can tell their own arithmetic slips apart from real hidden ambiguities, fixing the first and splitting the second, instead of averaging them away or repeating the same wrong fix.

IDEA 2
NAME: Residual-Deficit Premise Promotion
MECHANISM: Reasoning runs on reversible operations; the forward pass logs bits it cannot reverse in a residual register (added content), and the backward pass from answer plus premises measures input-reconstruction deficit (assumed content). Residual spans are decoded into candidate premise sentences and fed into the backward pass. A candidate becomes a stated assumption only if it closes a matching part of the deficit. Residual that closes no deficit is labelled fabricated, and deficit left unexplained triggers retrieval or a question to the user. Training minimises the bits left unmatched on both sides.
VALUE: Hidden assumptions and made-up claims are worked out mechanically and turned into explicit premises that can be tested, rather than being guessed from confidence scores.
