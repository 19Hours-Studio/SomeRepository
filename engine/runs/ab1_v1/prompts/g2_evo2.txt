PAIR 1
 Parent A: MECHANISM: Each reasoning step is treated as a survey observation with a variance. The planner deliberately builds redundancy by deriving key quantities along independent routes so the steps form closed loops. Least-squares adjustment spreads each loop's misclosure across the steps, and Baarda's w-test flags the step with the largest standardized residual. Only that step is re-derived, and the planner adds redundancy where the redundancy numbers show steps that cannot be checked.
   VALUE: Long chains locate their own blunders and design themselves to be checkable, so errors do not silently compound.
   nearest published work: A half century of Baarda's concept of reliability: a review (2018) https://doi.org/10.1080/00396265.2018.1548118 ; Temporal Consistency for LLM Reasoning Process Error Identification (2025) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.14495
 Parent B: MECHANISM: The model learns transformation maps between concepts and contexts. It decomposes them by Hodge decomposition to find cycles whose sum is not zero. Where curl persists, it adds a new latent context dimension and splits the concept along it until the cycles close. The split concepts are stored with the cycle that triggered them.
   VALUE: The model finds ambiguities it was not told about and gives each meaning its own concept, instead of averaging two incompatible ones.
   nearest published work: Statistical Ranking and Combinatorial Hodge Theory / HodgeRank (2008) https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.1067 ; On the Quantum-like Contextuality of Ambiguous Phrases (2021) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.14589

PAIR 2
 Parent A: MECHANISM: Each derivation step is built from operations that can be run in reverse. Information a step cannot reverse is written to an explicit residual register instead of being thrown away. After producing an output, the system runs the chain backward to rebuild the inputs. Residual bits that the inputs cannot account for mark exactly the spans where new content was added rather than derived. Training lowers the count of unexplained residual bits for each claim, and outputs are labelled by how much residual they carry.
   VALUE: Each statement can be checked mechanically to show whether it follows from the given material or was made up.
   nearest published work: i-RevNet: Deep Invertible Networks (2018) https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.07088 ; Large Language Models are Better Reasoners with Self-Verification (2022) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.09561 (also RevNet, Gomez et al. 2017 https://papers.NeurIPS.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/f9be311e65d81a9ad8150a60844bb94c-Paper.pdf; Bennett-style reversible computing, from memory, not surfaced in search)
 Parent B: MECHANISM: Reasoning runs on a bijective (invertible-layer) network that keeps the full input-plus-scratch state rather than throwing information away. After producing an answer, the system runs the computation backward from the answer and its stated premises only, then measures how much of the original input it can reconstruct. Low reconstruction means the answer depended on something the premises do not contain, so the gap is surfaced as an unstated assumption. Training penalises those gaps, which pushes the model to make every load-bearing assumption explicit.
   VALUE: Hidden assumptions become detectable as missing information rather than through confidence scores, so it becomes visible when a conclusion really depends on something unsaid.
   nearest published work: i-RevNet: Deep Invertible Networks (2018) https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.07088 ; Reverse Thinking Makes LLMs Stronger Reasoners (2024) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.19865v1 (also Self-Verification (2022) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.09561)
