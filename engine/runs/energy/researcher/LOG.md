# Research log: multi-day storage, focusing on the converter side (the hard part)

Data: German daily consumption, wind and solar 2012-2017 (OPSD via github.com/jenfly/opsd), stored in data/. Each source is normalised by its trailing-year mean and the system is scaled to a 100% wind+solar grid (s02).
Baseline (s01, s02): with the converter sized to the daily-mean peak deficit (61 GW; mean load is 56 GW), it runs about 900 full-load h/yr at OVER=1.3, and about 670 h/yr at OVER=1.5. At that duty, a new H2 turbine adds 0.10-0.19 $/kWh and an rSOC adds 0.35-0.7 $/kWh. Converter capex is the dominant cost, which confirms the hard part.
Charge side (s03): even at RTE 0.18, enough surplus can be captured at OVER=1.5 with a 55 GW charge converter at CF 0.47. At OVER=1.3, an RTE below about 0.25 cannot be supplied at all. So low RTE costs overbuild, while the discharge kW is what dominates.
