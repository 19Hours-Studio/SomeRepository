=== VALUED C01 ===
ANCHOR: two unmined ore lenses as electrodes
NAME: Contained Magnetite Ore-Lens Battery
MECHANISM: Two buried magnetite lenses with barren rock between them become the two electrodes. A borehole ring injects a carbonate-buffered alkaline brine so that both charged states stay in place as insoluble solids: magnetite oxidizes to maghemite/hematite on one side and reduces to ferrous hydroxide/green rust on the other. Nothing dissolves, migrates or becomes acid drainage. The same perimeter boreholes pump a slight inward hydraulic gradient, which keeps oxygen-bearing outside water from discharging the store. Self-potential mapping shows where the charged front lags, and current is moved between boreholes to keep the front even. Because each lens spreads the reaction over millions of square metres of mineral surface, current density stays tiny and voltage losses stay small. The only fabricated converter is the inverter.
VALUE: Owners of magnetite deposits and flooded iron-mine voids gain a reversible, non-polluting seasonal store whose cost per kWh is drilling plus an inverter, aimed at utilities that need to cover multi-week lulls.

=== VALUED C02 ===
ANCHOR: charged molecules precipitate as strained insoluble isomers
NAME: Precipitating-Isomer Ditch Cell
MECHANISM: A dissolved organic molecule is reduced, and a partner molecule is oxidized, into strained isomers that crystallize after a delay, so they nucleate on seed crystals in a downstream settling basin rather than on the electrodes. Charging and discharging both happen in one membrane-free open channel lined with carbon. The electrolyte stays at a fixed saturated composition, so the voltage stays flat. The carbon floor carries a potential-gated catalyst that speeds up the snap back only when the floor is held at discharge potential. As a result, the same cheap area charges without reverting its own product and discharges by redissolving slurry fed back up the channel. The two powders sit on covered heaps for months. The converter is a lined ditch run at very low current density, so it is both cheap and low-loss.
VALUE: Grid operators get weeks of storage where the bulk medium and the single bidirectional converter both cost about as much as earthworks, with no crossover and no electrode fouling.

=== VALUED C03 ===
ANCHOR: air-to-peroxide powder through one reversible cell
NAME: Peroxide Plant With Power Reserve
MECHANISM: A single membraneless carbon cell array reduces air oxygen to peroxide in soda-ash solution. The peroxide is crystallized into sodium percarbonate and stored in silos. In normal months the cell runs as a merchant electrochemical peroxide plant, ramping up on cheap surplus power and selling percarbonate to pulp-bleaching, water-treatment and detergent buyers. A strategic silo stockpile is held back and, during a dark calm spell, discharged through the same cell back to oxygen and water. Because the converter earns chemical revenue at a high capacity factor, its capex is not stranded across a year with few cycles. Low discharge efficiency matters less because the stockpile was made from near-zero-price power and costs only powder and silo space.
VALUE: Regions with surplus renewables gain a chemical business whose inventory doubles as a weeks-long power reserve, so storage converter capex is paid by product sales rather than by rare discharges.

=== VALUED C04 ===
ANCHOR: paper-machine carbon-on-cellulose electrode area
NAME: Spiral-Wound Paper Flow Cartridges
MECHANISM: A paper machine lays down carbon electrodes and a cellulose separator in one pass at kilometres per minute. The laminate is then wound with mesh spacers into spiral cartridges like reverse-osmosis elements, so thousands of square metres fit into one mass-produced pressure tube with no frames, bipolar plates or gaskets. The cartridges are oversized five to ten times and paired with a fast outer-sphere redox couple that has no oxygen-evolution step. At that low current density the voltage loss is mostly ohmic and falls in proportion, giving high round-trip efficiency without catalysts or ion-exchange membranes. Spent cartridges are re-pulped to recover the carbon.
VALUE: Any cheap-tank flow chemistry gains a converter that is both near paper cost per square metre and highly efficient, removing the stack cost and loss that dominate long-duration storage economics.

=== VALUED C05 ===
ANCHOR: gravity-separated charged phases in a pond
NAME: Solar-Float Gravity-Separated Storage Pond
MECHANISM: When charged, a redox molecule binds a polyelectrolyte and sinks as a dense phase onto a carbon-felt pond floor, which is the positive electrode. A cheap metal plates onto a floating carbon mat. Gravity and the phase boundary replace the separator. The mat's buoyancy holds it a constant few centimetres above the rising dense layer, which keeps the ionic path, and so the ohmic loss, small. Photovoltaic modules are mounted on the floating mat and feed DC straight into the pond, so charging needs no inverter. Electrode area automatically equals panel area, so current density stays low and losses small over a 10-day discharge.
VALUE: Solar farms gain week-long storage inside the land they already occupy, with a cell that costs about as much as a pond liner and a float.

=== VALUED C06 ===
ANCHOR: natural smectite structural iron as electrode
NAME: All-Clay Pit Battery
MECHANISM: Two excavated beds form the cell: iron-rich smectite clay, whose layer iron switches between Fe(III) and Fe(II), as the negative bed, and cheap manganese-oxide ore as the positive bed. A compacted bentonite barrier layer, a standard landfill liner, separates them. A dissolved mediator in the pore water carries electrons from embedded carbon rods to every clay platelet, so beds metres thick work as electrodes at days-long discharge rates despite clay's insulation. Electrodes, separator and store are all bulk earth at tens of dollars per tonne. The only fabricated parts are carbon rods and an inverter.
VALUE: Utilities and landowners gain a weeks-long store built with earthmoving equipment from local clay, in which the electrode itself is the converter, so there is no stack capex to idle through the year.

=== VALUED C07 ===
ANCHOR: electron-balance residual by potential on raw brine
NAME: Brine-Fractionating Treatment-Storage Cascade
MECHANISM: Raw mine or industrial waste brine flows through a cascade of cheap cells, each held at a stepped electrode potential. The charge each stage passes, minus the conversion measured there, tells it which couple it is working on. Stages whose residual shows a parasitic couple precipitate it out. Stages whose residual shows a reversible couple, usually iron or manganese, split it into its own purified stream, which becomes a flow-battery electrolyte with no separate purification plant. When it is not storing power, the cascade keeps treating brine for remediation fees, so stack area earns money year-round. New stack area is added only where measured charge return justifies it.
VALUE: Mine operators and nearby grids gain storage electrolyte that is effectively free, made inside a water-treatment plant whose remediation revenue carries the converter capex.
