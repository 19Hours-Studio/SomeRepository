L01 | PARTIAL | Electrochemically controlled energy storage in a norbornadiene-based solar fuel with 99% reversibility (2019) https://research.chalmers.se/publication/511689 ; electrochemically triggered quadricyclane-to-norbornadiene conversion (ChemSusChem, 2016) https://air.unimi.it/handle/2434/471319 (also the liquid azobenzene MOST system whose back-isomerization is electron-catalysed, 2025, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12332809/) | Prior work charges the strained isomer with light and releases the stored energy electrochemically as heat; the new part is charging it electrically and recovering the release as electrons for grid storage. The 2016 voltammetry found the isomers' redox potentials differ by less than their neutral-state energy gap, which suggests round-trip efficiency would be a problem.
L02 | PARTIAL | Vanadium recovered from fly ash or waste streams and formulated into redox flow battery electrolyte (US10422021 and similar, ~2019) https://patents.google.com/patent/US10422021 ; acid mine drainage tested as a galvanic-cell electrolyte (FIRST-ESCSI-22 proceedings, 2022) https://www.atlantis-press.com/proceedings/first-escsi-22/125987962 (also online multi-species state observers for unbalanced vanadium flow batteries, 2023-24) | Not found: an unpurified multi-couple waste brine used directly as the storage medium, with an electron-balance residual that finds unknown couples and sends them to their own stacks or precipitation. Existing work either purifies the waste first or monitors known species.
L03 | NO-CLOSE-MATCH | Imprisoned Lightning: charge transport in trehalose-derived sugar glasses (Ionics, 2015) https://scholars.nova.edu/en/publications/imprisoned-lightning-charge-transport-in-trehalose-derived-sugar--2/ ; redox-targeting "solid boosters" in flow batteries (2021) https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8067695/ | I found nothing on vitrifying a charged redox electrolyte in a sugar glass and redissolving it later. Trehalose glasses are reported to support long-range redox electron transfer between donors and acceptors through water-wire proton hopping, which cuts against the premise that the glass freezes self-discharge.
L04 | PARTIAL | Sato & Mooney sulfide self-potential "geobattery" model (1960), cited and applied in the Sunrise deposit AUV survey (2018) https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40623-018-0913-6 ; in-situ electrochemical mining of sulfide ore bodies (US3957601, 1976) https://patents.justia.com/patent/3957601 (also subsurface electrolyte storage, https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/en/publications/subsurface-flow-batteries-concept-benefits-and-hurdles) | The natural geobattery and driving current through ore are both known; the new part is deliberately cycling a natural ore body as a rechargeable storage electrode, steered by self-potential. In the Sato-Mooney picture the ore only conducts electrons and does not itself react, and the mining patents report sulfur passivation, so both are cautions for the idea.
L05 | PARTIAL | Road based electrical storage batteries (US11788241, ~2023) https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11788241 ; Chalmers rechargeable cement-based Fe-Ni battery (2023) https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/researchers-develop-rechargeable-cement-based-battery/ (also MIT cement-carbon black supercapacitor, https://www.bdcnetwork.com/concrete/concrete-technology/news/55166232/mit-researchers-are-working-on-a-way-to-use-concrete-as-an-electric-battery) | Batteries embedded in roads and in cement are known; the new part is redox slag aggregate with a scarcity-price-driven drawdown held to a structural depletion limit by load-bearing monitoring. The Chalmers prototype reached only about 7 Wh/m2, so capacity is a doubt.
L06 | PARTIAL | Gravity-driven, pump- and membrane-free zinc-bromine flow battery using density stratification (Mohr, Columbia dissertation, ~2022) https://ceec.engineering.columbia.edu/events/rob-mohr-ceec-phd-dissertation-defense ; membraneless energy storage with immiscible electrolyte solutions (2017) https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/items/2961cf1f-ae8c-401e-82c4-c064558cfe79 (also Ambri/MIT liquid metal battery, self-segregating layers, https://energy.mit.edu/news/a-battery-of-molten-metals) | Density-stratified batteries with no membrane are established. Not found: a solar-pond-style salt-gradient non-convective zone used as the ion-conducting separator in an earthwork pond. Redox-species crossover through that gradient zone, plus the upkeep solar ponds need to keep the gradient, would be the main risks.
NOTE: Most ideas combine a known mechanism with a new application or a new control scheme. Only L03 turned up no direct precedent, and the one nearby paper on trehalose glasses points against it. L01 and L04 have the closest precedents in strained-isomer electrochemistry and the geobattery. In several cases (L01, L04, L05) the literature also shows a practical limit the idea does not mention: poor redox-potential match, passivation, and low areal energy.
