=== ATTEMPTS ===
Only 15 searches were run. I list a figure only when a search result gave it, and I flag vendor claims, leaks and unsourced background. No independent crease-depth number in µm was found for any phone.

ATTEMPT 1: Thick UTG (30 µm, then 50% thicker), titanium lattice backplate and wider hinge wingplate | Samsung Fold 7, 2025 | standard | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: a faint line is still visible. The depth (~0.7 mm per your brief) has no public measurement. Samsung's next panel is reported at only ~20% less depth, and outlets disagree on whether that is depth or visibility | [Sammobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/inside-the-galaxy-z-fold-7-samsung-reveals-what-makes-it-stronger-lighter-thinner/), [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/01/20/news-samsung-reportedly-achieves-20-crease-reduction-with-utg-galaxy-z-fold-8-apple-weigh-adoption)

ATTEMPT 2: Laser-drilled (perforated) metal support plate under the OLED, with possible second UTG | Samsung Display "Advanced Crease-less" at CES 2026; reported for Apple's foldable, with Fine M-Tec as supplier | pilot or leak, not shipped | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: it is unconfirmed (leaks and analysts only). Sources disagree on the plate supplier, and TrendForce says Apple's patents alone are unlikely to give a fully crease-free design. No review unit exists, so persistence is unknown | [Droid-Life](https://www.droid-life.com/2026/01/06/samsung-crease-less-panel/), [Channelnews](https://www.channelnews.com.au/apple-taps-samsung-display-for-crease-free-iphone-fold-screen/)

ATTEMPT 3: Oppo Find N6 "Auto-Smoothing Flex Glass" (50% thicker than UTG), laser-scanned titanium hinge, and 3D "liquid printing" of hinge surfaces | Oppo, MWC 2026 | standard (flagship) | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: the claim is only –80 to –82% crease depth against the N5, a relative figure with no µm value. Hinge height variance falls from 0.2 mm to 0.05 mm, so the hinge still has tolerance. The 600,000-fold figure is a vendor claim from a TÜV "Minimized" certificate, and I found no independent teardown | [Oppo](https://www.oppo.com/en/newsroom/stories/oppo-find-n6-zero-feel-crease-foldable-innovation/), [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/oppo_details_find_n6s_impressive_creasefree_display-news-71887.php)

ATTEMPT 4: Thinner or more recoverable OCA, in various forms: high creep recovery of 90% or more, lower thickness, and a crosslink gradient | 3M, Samsung and Chinese patents, 2018–25 | standard | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: standard OCA needs hours to recover after a fold, and thicker layers recover more slowly. The failure mode is trade-offs: high creep gives buckling and cohesion failure under heat and humidity. The 3M target is 100,000 dynamic folds at 1–5 mm radius, which tests survival, not flatness | [3M whitepaper](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1793258O/optically-clear-adhesives-for-foldable-oled-displays-whitepaper.pdf), [patent](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11334117)

ATTEMPT 5: 30 µm chemically strengthened UTG, with Corning, Schott and NEG as suppliers | Samsung Display UTG (Bureau Veritas, 200,000 folds), NEG Dinorex (3 mm fold diameter, 200,000 cycles) | standard | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: it solves fracture, not creasing. Fatigue life drops steeply with thickness, so thickening the glass hurts. The polymer layers beneath still deform, and those tests do not measure crease | [Display Daily](https://displaydaily.com/with-ultra-thin-glass-schott-leads-the-way), [Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/us/samsung-utg-glass-foldable-display-easily-bendable-ultra-thin-glass), [Mobility Arena](https://mobilityarena.com/what-is-dinorex-utg/)

ATTEMPT 6: Teardrop or water-drop hinge that gives a larger fold radius (a gap in the folded phone instead of a sharp bend) | Honor, Oppo, Samsung, 2022–25 | standard | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: it needs thickness. The Fold 7 is 8.66 mm folded, and the radius is capped by that thickness. Hinge tolerances still show up as a line (see 0.2 mm → 0.05 mm in Attempt 3) | [Sammobile measurement](https://www.androidauthority.com/foldable-phone-thickness-study-galaxy-z-fold-7-thinner-3580989)

ATTEMPT 7: Self-healing or shape-memory cover layer, with transparent heater layers that return a dent to flat | Apple patent; Motorola patent; KIST elastomer | niche (patents and papers only) | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: it heals scratches, not the bulk creep of PI, OCA and the support plate. Apple's patent gives no temperature. A shape-memory polymer needs ~60–70 °C for tens of seconds to minutes (a patent figure for packaging, not displays), which is above phone safe limits. The KIST elastomer heals scratches in 10 s and survives 200,000 folds at 1.5 mm, but that says nothing about the crease | [Apple patent](https://www.androidauthority.com/apple-self-healing-foldable-screen-patent-3445460), [Motorola](https://www.slashgear.com/motorolas-planning-a-thermal-self-repairing-phone-15495184/)

ATTEMPT 8: Outward fold with a very large radius | Royole FlexPai 2018, Huawei Mate X 2019 | dropped | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: the FlexPai had a "very visible" crease. The screen was permanently exposed, and the Mate X had screen problems after multiple folds | [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/the-royole-flexpai-is-improved-but-still-doesnt-feel-like-a-galaxy-fold-rival), [Techspot](https://www.techspot.com/trivia/180-what-first-consumer-gadget-feature-foldable-display/)

ATTEMPT 9: Two separate screens with a hinge gap (no folding panel) | Microsoft Surface Duo, 2020 | dropped or niche | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: there is no crease, but there is a hard seam, and users complained the gap makes maximised apps unusable. Early units reported hinge problems. This fails the "one continuous display" requirement | [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/surface-duo-receives-update-as-hinge-problems-impact-the-phone_id127648)

ATTEMPT 10: Rollable or slidable display, with a 5–15 mm radius and no sharp fold | BOE, CSoT, Samsung Display; LG (cancelled, with its phone business); Oppo and Motorola concepts | pilot or dropped | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: it needs a minimum radius above ~7 mm for a 100 µm panel to keep strain under 1%. Samsung's own paper still reports edge deformation of up to 4 mm after 200,000 rolls at a 15 mm radius. Neither CSoT's rollable phone panel (3 mm radius, 100,000 sliding cycles) nor BOE's 5 mm (100,000 bends) has been shipped in a phone | [OLED-Info](https://www.oled-info.com/boe-demonstrates-15-rollable-oled-display), [Display Daily](https://displaydaily.com/visionox-to-discuss-7-2-rollable-amoled-reliability/), [patent](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8001711)

ATTEMPT 11: Multi-fold with several hinges and a shared folding display | Galaxy Z TriFold, Dec 2025 | standard but expensive | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: the result is two indentations, visible under bright light when looked for. In an unofficial test the hinges lost rigidity at ~144,000 folds against a 200,000 rating | [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/12/23/galaxy-z-trifold-hinge-torture-test/)

ATTEMPT 12: First-generation protective layers and sealed hinges | Galaxy Fold 2019 | dropped | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: peel-off layers and debris in the hinge killed review units within days. This failure was about mechanical protection, not about creasing | [Techspot](https://www.techspot.com/trivia/180-what-first-consumer-gadget-feature-foldable-display/)

ATTEMPT 13: Foldable PC with a thicker panel and large radius | ThinkPad X1 Fold, 2020 | niche | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: a "slight crease" was visible at an angle (Counterpoint, via Display Daily). A bigger panel and radius cost thickness and mass. No µm figure | [Display Daily](https://displaydaily.com/?p=76818)

ATTEMPT 14: Reliability testing that counts folds | whole industry: 200,000 to 600,000 folds | standard | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: it checks for breakage, not crease. No public µm profilometry exists for any phone, so "crease-free" can't be verified from the outside | [Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/us/samsung-utg-glass-foldable-display-easily-bendable-ultra-thin-glass)

ATTEMPT 15: Moving the neutral plane to the OLED layer by matching layer moduli and thickness | all OEMs | standard | WHY IT DID NOT SOLVE IT: sources say creases come from neutral-plane misalignment, but it can only be balanced for one fold state. The other layers still creep, and the result is relative reduction, not zero | [TechBuzz](https://techbuzzireland.com/2026/08/11/crease-to-visually-seamless-technology-path-to-crease-control-in-foldables/)

LESSONS:
- Every shipped fix is a relative reduction (–20%, –80%, –82%), with no absolute µm values. A crease-free claim needs profilometry in µm, which is unpublished.
- The polymer and adhesive layers set the crease, not the glass. Glass tests prove survival, not flatness.
- A fix that works at one fold radius or temperature fails at another. The healing approaches (Attempt 7) need heat beyond phone-safe limits.
- Larger radius (teardrop hinge, rollable) works, but costs thickness. The only no-radius approach, a gap, fails the continuous-screen requirement.
- Nobody has published crease persistence after 2,000 folds in the heat and humidity of a pocket.
