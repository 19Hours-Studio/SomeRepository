IDEA 1: Centre-pinned capillary-wrung glass sandwich
DEFECT | FATAL | The 14 kPa capillary clamp can't exist with an oil-filled reservoir. The suction is set by the meniscus with the largest gap, which is the 0.5 mm groove, not the 3 µm film. Gravity then drains the groove. | Groove suction 2γ/h = 2·0.021/0.5e-3 = 84 Pa, which is 167× less than 14 kPa. The oil column in an upright phone exerts ρgh = 1070·9.81·0.15 = 1.57 kPa, about 19× the groove suction, so oil runs to the bottom of the groove and is pushed out there or forces the layers apart. With p = 84 Pa, the model's own formula w = Dκ₀²/2p gives 8.7 µm of lift at 5% set and 35 µm at 10% set, not 52 nm and 209 nm (python, D_OLED = 7.2e-6 N·m).
DEFECT | MAJOR | The plates must sit 20 µm below the stack and the hard stops must be trimmed to ≤3.5 µm. Both are tighter than the best hinge flatness anyone has shown. | Oppo N6's per-unit 3D-scan and UV-polymer correction gets height variation to 0.05 mm, against an industry norm of 0.2 mm. So the requirement is 14× tighter than best practice, and the 20 µm gap is smaller than the 50 µm variation, so the plates will touch the stack and print through.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The 0.02° hard-stop allowance takes up the whole slope budget for visibility on its own. | 0.02° = 0.35 mrad, which is more than the ≤0.3 mrad target used in idea 4. That leaves no margin for OLED set, thermal effects or wear.
DEFECT | MINOR | The design drops the polarizer but does not specify colour filter on encapsulation, so ambient light reflects off the bare OLED metal. | Order-of-magnitude figure, not sourced in this run: roughly 40% reflectance without a polarizer, versus about 4–8% with a polarizer or colour filter on encapsulation. Idea 2 does specify colour filter on encapsulation.
DEFECT | MINOR | The glass stress margin assumes the bend radius is 3.5 mm everywhere. Waterdrop necks bend tighter. | At R = 2.6 mm, a 50 µm cover glass sees 692 MPa, which is at the ≥700 MPa compressive surface stress, so the 186 MPa margin is gone.
DEFECT | MINOR | A bare 50 µm cover glass on 3 µm of oil, with no polymer protector, has no cushioning and no film to hold shards. A 2000-cycle test sample size is not stated. | Samsung uses a protector film and Apple a replaceable top layer. With n = 1 and zero failures, the 95% lower bound on reliability is only 5%; 95%/95% needs n = 59.
SURVIVES | Fold-slip values of 64 µm and 112 µm (π/2·d), OLED bend strain 0.36%, cover glass 514 MPa at E = 72 GPa, 2γ/h = 14 kPa for a 3 µm film, stack 111 µm, cost 3.3–11.5% of $122. The cover glass is 110× stiffer than the OLED, which partly isolates the front surface from OLED lift.
RATIO | 0.93 | $122 / ($122 + ~$9) inner-display subsystem, before the unpriced per-unit trimming to ≤3.5 µm, which would lower it further. The baseline is the Oppo N6 / Fold 8 class, not Apple's matte approach.
KNOWN | partly | A fluid interlayer between the cover window and the panel is already patented: US 2021/0358341 and US 11,308,827 ("viscoelastic fluid layers", ≤25 µm).
NEW FACT | Oppo N6: per-hinge 3D scan plus UV-cured polymer droplets cut height variation from 0.2 mm to 0.05 mm; the waterdrop is 11% wider; the flex glass is 50% thicker than standard UTG | GSMArena / AndroidHeadlines, Mar 2026

IDEA 2: Fluid-slip glass-leaf sandwich
DEFECT | MAJOR | The stated slope and depth don't reproduce. A stack that is held together in the normal direction but slides freely keeps a kink of set × π × D-ratio. | At 10% set: 0.1·π·0.009 = 2.83 mrad with 3 leaves and 1.57 mrad with 5 leaves, against the claimed ≤0.27 mrad. Depth κs²/8 over an arc of πR = 7.9 mm is 2.8 µm and 1.5 µm, against the claimed 0.3–0.7 µm. That is 5–10× above the 0.3 mrad visibility target, and the "70× flatter than Duo" becomes 21.7/2.83 to 21.7/1.57, i.e. 8–14×.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The panel set of ≤10% is assumed, not sourced, and the result scales linearly with it. | At 30% set: 4.7–8.5 mrad. No set data is given for a 20 µm colour-filter-on-encapsulation panel after 2000 folds at 60 °C or after long holds folded.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The sealed silicone fluid spread over 5–7 films changes volume with temperature and holds dissolved air, so bubbles can form and remain visible. Permeation through a soft seal is not addressed. | Volumetric thermal expansion of about 9.5e-4 /K gives 7.6% volume change over 80 K. At −20 °C the fluid goes into suction. Silicone fluids typically hold about 15–20% air by volume (order of magnitude, not sourced in this run).
DEFECT | MINOR | The 69-day squeeze-flow "lock" contradicts free slip. Low viscosity gives a lock of hours at most; high viscosity resists the fold. In the contact limit the D-ratio result still holds, so this is minor. | With μ = 1 Pa·s, a 20 mm radius and 1 N, a 5 µm film halves in 0.5 days. Getting 69 days needs about 130 Pa·s, which gives about 80 N of drag per interface during a 0.5 s fold and about 0.3% membrane strain in the panel.
DEFECT | MINOR | D-ratio of 0.005 for 5 leaves is not consistent with 0.009 for 3 leaves. Breakage risk is not addressed for 4–6 glass sheets each at 420 MPa. | The modulus that gives 0.009 for 3 leaves (E_p = 8.6 GPa) gives 0.0063 for 5 leaves. Under weakest-link statistics, breakage probability scales by about 4–6×. The bottom leaf slips 346 µm at each end.
SURVIVES | UTG at 0.6% and 420 MPa, panel at 0.4% for R = 2.5 mm; the combined-curvature law κ = (D_p/ΣD)·κ₀ is correct; the BOM range of 3–9% matches $20–60 out of $670.
RATIO | 0.94 | $670 / ($670 + ~$40) Fold-class BOM; the panel-set risk is unpriced.
KNOWN | partly | Same fluid-interlayer patents: US 2021/0358341 and US 11,308,827. Extra UTG under the panel is a reported Fold 8 rumour (Croma, Jan 2026).
NEW FACT | iPhone Duo (HTX Studio, n = 1): 48 µm peak-to-valley when new, 167 µm after 3000 folds; slope 0.097° rising to 1.245° (21.7 mrad) | ithinkdiff / TechEBlog, Oct 2026

IDEA 3: Glass-carried thermally scheduled tension
DEFECT | FATAL | This is not a complete architecture. It only works on top of a base stack whose residual crease is ≤10 µm and stays there for 2000 cycles, and no such stack is specified or shown to exist. | The only verified measurement is Duo at 48 µm new and 167 µm after 3000 folds. Oppo's N6 figure (+22 µm after 600k folds) is Oppo's own claim, and the idea admits it can't handle Apple/Samsung/Oppo-sized creases.
DEFECT | MAJOR | At 60 °C the scheduled glass stretch is 5 µm (129 N/m). That is within the hinge's positional and wear tolerance, so the tension can fall to zero. | The 25 °C window of 90–2100 N/m corresponds to 3.5–81 µm of stretch. Demonstrated hinge tolerances are tens of µm (Oppo's best height variation is 50 µm). A ±5 µm error at 60 °C gives 0–258 N/m.
DEFECT | MINOR | The 522 N load held at −20 °C shortens the frame by a meaningful fraction of the stretch. | Two titanium rails of 8 mm² over 70 mm have stiffness 2.5e7 N/m, giving 21 µm of shortening, about 15% of the 135 µm stretch.
SURVIVES | The tension-filter formula is derived correctly; 239 MPa × 50 µm = 11,950 N/m and × 0.151 m = 1.8 kN; link 14.5 ppm/K × 56 mm × 2 = 1.62 µm/K; 70 MPa, 522 N and 135 mm all reproduce; 25 °C gives 61.7 µm = 1590 N/m, inside the window.
RATIO | 0.99 | As an add-on: $670 / ($670 + $5). The ratio means nothing until a base stack with ≤10 µm residual exists.
KNOWN | partly | Hinges with adjustable length or display tension appear in the patent literature (EP 4550765 background; Huawei spring-loaded support). Nothing shipping is known.
NEW FACT | Galaxy Z Fold 8 uses a "Flex Titanium" dual layer (titanium film plus titanium plate); a Korean report says the crease is about 20% shallower than Fold 7 | 9to5Google / SamMobile, 2026

IDEA 4: Load-cell-scheduled drumhead hinge
DEFECT | FATAL | The required input, a crease of about 48 µm with σ ≥ 8 mm, does not last 2000 cycles on the only measured stack. The crease deepens and narrows into the regime this idea itself labels infeasible. | Duo: 48 µm becomes 167 µm by 3000 folds, about 127 µm at 2000 by linear interpolation. Implied width σ ≈ 0.607·w/slope falls from 17 mm to 4.7 mm. The idea's own figure for 167 µm is 2.9 kN/m, which strains the OCA by about 670%.
DEFECT | MAJOR | D is not stated, and the claim only works for a stack bending stiffness of ≤ ~3e-4 N·m. | FFT filter run for a 48 µm, σ = 8 mm crease: D = 3e-4 gives 1.1–1.5 µm (passes); D = 1e-3 gives 3.3–4.5 µm and 0.37–0.50 mrad (fails); D = 3e-3 gives 8–10 µm. A 30 µm stainless support plate alone has D = 4.5e-4.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The 12 µm thermal shift per 40 K implies only 2.1 ppm/K of mismatch. A Fold-class aluminium frame against a glass-dominated stack gives 7× more. | 15 ppm/K × 140 mm × 40 K = 84 µm. Through the 1 kN/mm series spring that is an 84 N error against a 30–43 N target, so the actuator needs at least 84 µm of stroke.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The ≤50% limit on sustained OCA shear fails when hot. Hours of being held open then creep the OCA into a new set. | 290 N/m over 15 mm gives 19 kPa of shear. With OCA shear modulus G ≈ 10–20 kPa at 60 °C (assumed, not sourced in this run), shear strain is 97–190%.
DEFECT | MINOR | The 2–7 µm stretch doesn't reproduce if the glass is part of the tensioned stack. "No baseline at any cost" is not verifiable. | 290 N/m ÷ (70 GPa × 30 µm) × 140 mm = 19 µm. Oppo publishes no slope or curvature figures.
SURVIVES | 30–43 N over 150 mm, BOM share of 0.75–1.5%; the 20 µm, σ = 4.7 mm case reaches ≤2 µm if D ≤ 3e-4.
RATIO | 0.99 | $670 / ($670 + $7.5); load-cell calibration and the actuator are unpriced.
KNOWN | partly | Same tension-hinge patent background as idea 3. Oppo's per-unit correction is the closest shipping approach to measured flatness, but it is a static fix.
NEW FACT | Oppo states the N6 crease deepened by 22 µm after 600k folds, against 165 µm for Fold 7 (Oppo's figures as relayed by a reviewer, not verified); the reviewer could still feel the crease | nasilemaktech.com review
