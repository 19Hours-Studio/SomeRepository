IDEA 1 | I 7 | P 2 | DECIDES: reverse strain×time to cancel a 72 h/60 °C hold | Creep is nonlinear and the 2–4° overbend gives far lower curvature than the fold, so superposition can't cancel the set in seconds.
IDEA 2 | I 8 | P 5 | DECIDES: OCA shear creep under ~600 N/m (~90 N) held tension | The numbers check out (√(D/T)≈0.5 mm, glass strain 0.03%), but sustained tension can make the whole stack creep.
IDEA 3 | I 8 | P 4 | DECIDES: tension to flatten a 20 µm×3 mm set vs OCA edge-shear limit | Duplicates IDEA 2; load cells add cost without changing the physics.
IDEA 4 | I 7 | P 3 | DECIDES: polymer relaxing moment ÷ (glass+plate) stiffness ≥ 50× | This only moves the creep into open-state hours and builds in curl and shear stress, since the glass is laminated flat but the polymers curved.
IDEA 5 | I 9 | P 4 | DECIDES: fluid-gap uniformity ±1 µm over 2000 cycles at 45 °C | It removes the creep layer, but unbonded thin layers may buckle on the inner side, and fluid pumping affects touch and optics.
IDEA 6 | I 8 | P 5 | DECIDES: residual strain ≤0.05% after 8 h at 60 °C | This is the most robust physics (an elastic regime), but t/2R gives ≈0.6%, not 0.3%. Rails and an R5 mm barrel cost thickness. Rollables still deform at 15 mm.
IDEA 7 | I 8 | P 3 | DECIDES: outer-glass strain of a bonded ~90 µm glass-OLED-glass stack at R=2 mm (≈2%) | Bonded, the glass breaks. Decoupled with soft OCA, the shear creep returns.
IDEA 8 | I 9 | P 4 | DECIDES: edge-seal and fluid retention, gap ±0.5 µm over 2000 cycles | This is the most complete slip variant, including the plate. The risks are seal life, buckling and capacitive touch through fluid.
IDEA 9 | I 7 | P 3 | DECIDES: tension for 5 µm residual vs glass edge strength | 20–40 N/m gives √(D/T)≈2–3 mm, about the crease width, so the flattening is weak.
IDEA 10 | I 7 | P 2 | DECIDES: slope ≤0.5 mrad between ~0.5 mm-pitch actuators | Actuators in a bending zone and µm-accurate gauge sensing are implausible. It also supports from below, which can't remove the panel's own set.
IDEA 11 | I 9 | P 3 | DECIDES: TFT/encapsulation film cracking and fatigue at 0.75% over 2000 cycles | Touch, polarizer and encapsulation still bend. LTPS on 30 µm strengthened glass is unproven in a fab.
IDEA 12 | I 8 | P 4 | DECIDES: tension for ≤1 µm vs OCA shear creep and UTG edge stress | Duplicates IDEA 2, with a stricter target.
IDEA 13 | I 5 | P 2 | DECIDES: depth drift cycle 300→2300 ≤2 µm under 60 °C holds | The crease recovers and re-forms with dwell and temperature, so a static shim mismatches. It is an Oppo-style reduction.
IDEA 14 | I 8 | P 5 | DECIDES: residual depth under 0.1% tension (~2 kN/m glass, ~300 N total) vs edge-bond hold | It combines a sub-creep radius with flattening, but the bond load is high and the closed phone is +1.5 mm thicker.
IDEA 15 | I 9 | P 5 | DECIDES: gel residual shear set after 8 h folded at 60 °C | This is the best-posed slip test. Colour-filter-on-encapsulation is already shipping. Gel elasticity is the open question.
