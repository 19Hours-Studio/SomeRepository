IDEA 3: Append-only errata with ski-rental compaction
DEFECT | MAJOR | Sorting documents by content hash does not make document-set changes cheap. DEFECT | MAJOR | The baseline (editing old tool outputs in place) is not current practice; Manus-style append-only plus deterministic serialization is. DEFECT | MAJOR | The replay numbers do not reproduce at the stated 1.25x write price. The figures fit if writes were billed at 1.0x and errata are about 500 tokens. | My sim: at 1.0x, errata 361k and inline 512–583k; at 1.25x, errata 388k and inline 584–674k, depending on which old output is edited. That makes the gain 1.33–1.74x, i.e. a 25–42% saving.

IDEA 4: Context-length-gated turn collapsing
DEFECT | MAJOR | The 2.02x "gate only" figure on 150 turns is above the Amdahl bound. With reads at 75% of cost and k = 3 folding applied only after about 30k tokens (about 97% of read volume), the maximum is 1/(0.25 + 0.75 × (0.03 + 0.97/3)) ≈ 1.94x. | Reaching 2.02x needs savings outside reads that are neither stated nor sourced.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The cost per task ignores changes in success rate. Folding plus 50% output filtering removes information the agent can react to, and neither the success delta nor retry cost is measured. | Example: success falling from 60% to 52% raises cost per solved task 1.15x, which cuts 2.4x to about 2.1x.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The gating quantity, the "expected value of seeing the result", cannot be computed; there is no estimator, so the gate is a heuristic threshold on context length. | Nothing in the text says how it is computed.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The competing baselines are understated. Observation masking measured about a 50% cost cut against the raw agent at equal solve rate. Anthropic's code execution with MCP and programmatic tool calling already filter tool outputs in scripts. | [Complexity Trap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21433): about 2x, though its cache accounting is unverified. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/code-execution-with-mcp): 150k→2k tokens in one example. Third parties measured 78.5% (AIMultiple) and 37% (programmatic tool calling, secondary source).
DEFECT | MINOR | The CodeAct conversion is internally inconsistent: a 30% turn cut with reads at 75% of cost gives 1/(0.25 + 0.525) = 1.29x, not 1.20x. | Arithmetic.
SURVIVES | Restricting folding to idempotent tools and stopping early are sound safety rules.
RATIO | 1.2 | Gain over masking plus code-mode filtering, which already capture most of the turn and observation reduction.
KNOWN | yes | CodeAct (Wang et al., ICML 2024); Anthropic programmatic tool calling and code execution with MCP (Nov 2025).
