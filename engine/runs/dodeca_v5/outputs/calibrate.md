=== CALIBRATION ===

P | 1 | 40% | Widest fit (11/16). It explains core holes, axis pairing (p = 0.0005), the unringed through-axis, unworn shells and contexts. It loses weight for having no test, a weak zodiac link (Geneva is an inscribed lead die) and an unexplained distribution.

P | 2 | 14% | Explains the pierced faces, pairing, thin walls and lack of wear without assuming use. It openly fails on the unringed larger or oval pair, and its map argument is circular. It is the natural fallback if the through-axis turns out not to be functional.

P | 4 | 9% | Shares Idea 2's casting strengths. But "freehand" hole sizes contradict the measured axis matching, the knobs-as-vents claim is unsupported, and its refutation test needs two conditions at once.

P | 3 | 5% | Matched opposite holes leave about six distinguishable outcomes, not twelve. The knob argument is wrong, the test cannot refute it, and the Geneva die suggests regional lots were inscribed.

P | none | 32% | No candidate explains the north-western distribution or the 2.5× size spread inside one object as design with a purpose. No workshop is known, and every idea scores 11/16 or less. An unproposed function, or a mix of decorative and practical use, remains quite possible.

WEAKEST LINK: The staff-mount rests mainly on one bone object lying beside one piece (Gellep) and on the unringed pair being larger. No dodecahedron has been found actually fixed to a rod. The cosmological meaning is an add-on that explains none of the physical features.

FLIP TEST: Microscopy or CT of the rims of the unringed pair against the ringed holes, across roughly 20 or more pieces, plus residue analysis for wood or bone. | If the unringed rims show no fitting abrasion, burnish or organic residue that the ringed holes lack, and no piece shows a socket or fixing trace, Idea 1 drops below Idea 2 or "none". If such marks do appear consistently, Idea 1 rises above about 60%.

UNKNOWN: Even if they were mounted on staffs, we still would not know what the object meant, who carried it or on what occasions, or why the tradition stayed in the north-west.
