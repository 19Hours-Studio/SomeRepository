=== REVIEW ===
IDEA 1: Fluid-slip glass-leaf sandwich
DEFECT | MAJOR | The headline crease does not reproduce. With the stated stiffness ratio (0.009 for 3 leaves), 10% set over a bend of πR = 7.85 mm gives several times the claimed 0.3–0.7 µm and ≤0.27 mrad. | κ_eff = 40 m⁻¹ × 0.0088 = 0.35 m⁻¹, so depth is 2.7 µm and edge slope 1.4 mrad (range 1.1–3.4 µm and 0.6–1.7 mrad for E_panel 5–10 GPa and 3–5 leaves). The "70× flatter than the Duo at 3,000 folds" becomes 13–25×. That is no better than the *new* Duo's reported slope of <0.1° (1.75 mrad).
DEFECT | MAJOR | The "invisible" target has never been checked against what the eye can resolve. On a glossy surface, a reflected straight edge kinks by twice the surface slope. | 2 × 0.27 mrad = 1.9 arcmin; 2 × 1.4 mrad = 9.6 arcmin. Vernier/curvature hyperacuity is about 0.1–0.2 arcmin, so these are 10–100× above threshold.
DEFECT | MAJOR | A sealed silicone fill expands or shrinks by about 7% across the operating temperature range, and it holds dissolved air. Without a compliance and degassing design that the text doesn't give, you get cavitation or bubbles in the optical path. Any bubble over 50 µm is a visible defect. | β ≈ 9×10⁻⁴ /K × 80 K (−20 to 60 °C) = 7.2% of fluid volume. Mineral oil dissolves about 9 vol% air at 1 atm ([HAWE](https://www.hawe.com/fluid-lexicon/solubility-of-gas-in-a-hydraulic-fluid/)), and PDMS dissolves more (Dow EP0225071).
DEFECT | MAJOR | The outermost surface is bare 30 µm UTG with no polymer film, and there is no adhesive to hold fragments together. A crack therefore leaks fluid and releases loose shards. No shipping foldable uses bare UTG as the touch surface. | Samsung uses a film on its UTG, and Apple uses a peelable top layer ($19). The 3–5 leaves below are bare glass too.
DEFECT | MINOR | The "69 days under a finger press" squeeze time doesn't reproduce. A local press only has to move fluid about 5 mm, so the normal "lock" holds for seconds, not days. Under the panel's own set moment (about 27 Pa), the films redistribute in about 3 days. | Reynolds squeeze: μ = 0.134 Pa·s, a = 5 mm, F = 1 N, 3→2.7 µm takes 5 s and 3→1.5 µm takes 66 s.
SURVIVES | UTG 30 µm at R = 2.5 mm gives 0.6% strain and 420–432 MPa; the panel sees 0.4%; D_panel/D_total = 0.009 (3 leaves) and 0.006 (5 leaves) reproduce with E_panel ≈ 8 GPa.
RATIO | 0.94 | +$20–60 on the implied ~$670 BOM; the warranty exposure of bare glass is not included.
KNOWN | partly | Huawei Mate XT uses a UTG plus non-Newtonian-fluid laminate ([propakistani/stuff](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/08/huaweis-second-tri-fold-improves-screen-protection-with-new-inward-folding-design/)). No fully fluid-decoupled leaf stack has shipped.
NEW FACT | HTX measured the new iPhone Duo at 48 µm with a slope <0.1°, and at 167 µm and 1.24° after 3,000 folds. The phones were left closed before the rescan. | [ithinkdiff](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/iphone-duo-crease-test-3000-folds/), [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/apple-iphone-duo-foldable-display-crease-durability-tests-3726691/)

IDEA 2: Outward fold, 5.5 mm spine radius
DEFECT | MAJOR | The exposed, scratched surface is held in bending tension for every hour the phone is closed. The net stress turns tensile only a few µm below the surface, so an ordinary pocket scratch reaches fracture-level tension. | 460 MPa against CS 750–900 MPa with DOL 10–12 µm: net tension starts at 3.8–5.5 µm and reaches 480–540 MPa at the DOL. A 10 µm flaw gives K ≈ 3 MPa√m, against K_IC ≈ 0.7.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The crease physics "close to Idea 1" assumes the glass bends about its own midplane, which needs a soft decoupling interlayer. That brings back the adhesive-shear set the idea doesn't model (see Idea 4). | The OCA shear set estimate under Idea 4 is 30–300 µm.
DEFECT | MINOR | The cross-references are wrong: Idea 1 uses R = 2.5 mm with 30 µm leaves, so it does not need a bulb to reach 5.5 mm. "The same stack" is therefore undefined. | Text of Ideas 1 and 2.
SURVIVES | 70 µm at R = 5.5 mm gives 0.64% strain and 458 MPa; an 11 mm folded thickness matches the Mate X/Xs class.
RATIO | 0.97 | Similar BOM with no bulb hinge, but the whole screen is exposed when closed; replacement cost is not included.
KNOWN | yes | Huawei Mate X/Xs series; the outward segment of the Mate XT, which uses a UTG laminate (source above).

IDEA 3: Tensioned-flat hinge with non-contact backstop
DEFECT | MAJOR | A displacement-controlled cam can't hold 500–1000 N/m, because the stretch it must set is 6–12× smaller than the thermal mismatch between an aluminium frame and the glass. Tension would swing from slack to several kN/m. This is fatal in an aluminium-frame phone unless a constant-force spring is added, and none is specified. | Required stretch = T/ΣEt × 150 mm = 8–16 µm. Mismatch = 15 ppm/K × 150 mm × 45 K = 101 µm. Hinge height tolerance (Oppo's best) is 50 µm.
DEFECT | MAJOR | "Used with Idea 1" is self-contradictory. Idea 1's layers are pinned only at the centreline and slide freely at the far edges, so there is nothing to tension. Even if they could be tensioned, the stated gain is about 2× optimistic. | My calculation gives 1.7 µm → 0.45 µm (500 N/m) and 0.23 µm (1000 N/m), against the claimed 0.1–0.2 µm.
DEFECT | MINOR | The 10–20 µm non-contact gap is about the same size as the tensioned residual dent of a Fold-like stack, so a downward dent can still land on the plates and print through. | 6–12 µm dent against a 10–20 µm gap.
SURVIVES | ℓ = 1.5–2.1 mm; 7–14 MPa in the glass; 75–150 N total; a 1 N press deflects 0.12–0.34 mm over a 6–10 mm span; Oppo's 0.05 mm hinge figure is confirmed ([GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/oppo_details_find_n6s_impressive_creasefree_display-news-71887.php)). I could not confirm US 12487643.
RATIO | 0.99 | A few dollars of hinge parts on a ~$670 BOM.
KNOWN | partly | Hinge patents already describe applying a "predetermined tension" to the display when unfolded and relaxing it when folded (e.g. [US11644873](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11644873)).

IDEA 4: Glass-dominated elastic stack, 4 mm teardrop
DEFECT | MAJOR | The model leaves out adhesive shear set, which is the mechanism Oppo itself names as the cause of creases. A 25 µm OCA has to take about 460% shear when folded. If 10% of that is not recovered, the residual tractions bend the stack 15–150× more than the claimed crease. | Slip = 72.5 µm × π/2 = 114 µm per side. τ = G × 0.46 = 4.6–46 kPa, giving κ = 1.5–15 m⁻¹ and a depth of 30–300 µm, against the claimed 0.3–1.7 µm.
DEFECT | MAJOR | "The glass surface never goes into net tension" holds only for the top 2–4 µm. The convex surface, the cut edges and the reverse-curvature neck of the teardrop all have flaws deeper than that. | 630 MPa against CS ≥750 MPa (DOL 10–12 µm): net tension begins at 1.7–3.6 µm and reaches 600–650 MPa at the DOL, giving K ≈ 3.8 MPa√m for a 10 µm flaw.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The "calibration" is one unknown stack fitted with a φ range spanning 5×, and it still misses the data. | The model gives 13–66 µm; the Duo measured 48–167 µm, 2.5× above the model's maximum.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The ≤0.4 mrad visibility threshold is unvalidated. | 0.8 mrad of reflected deviation is 2.75 arcmin, about 15–30× hyperacuity.
DEFECT | MINOR | The design claims "no glassy polymer in the fold zone" but uses a CPI panel substrate. The "~11 µm N6" baseline has no source: Oppo publishes only an 82% reduction against the N5. The slope is understated about 2×. | My calculation gives 0.13–0.67 mrad against 0.07–0.35 mrad claimed ([Oppo](https://www.oppo.com/en/newsroom/stories/oppo-find-n6-zero-feel-crease-foldable-innovation/)).
DEFECT | MINOR | There is no sample plan. Zero failures in 10 units over 2000 cycles shows only ≥74% reliability at 95% confidence. | 0.05^(1/10) = 0.74.
SURVIVES | UTG 0.875%/630 MPa, panel 0.31%, β-Ti 0.5%/500 MPa, glass share of bending stiffness 78%; depth 0.42–2.1 µm for panel-only set.
RATIO | 0.98 | A thicker bare UTG with no film is cheaper, but a larger teardrop hinge plus glass-breakage warranty roughly cancels that.
KNOWN | partly | The Oppo Find N6 "Auto-Smoothing Flex Glass" is 50% thicker UTG used as a spring against adhesive creep, and Oppo says its effect "will decrease with prolonged usage" ([Oppo](https://www.oppo.com/en/newsroom/stories/oppo-find-n6-zero-feel-crease-foldable-innovation/)).

IDEA 5: Centre-pinned capillary-wrung glass sandwich
DEFECT | FATAL | An open 0.5 mm oil reservoir sets the pressure of the whole connected film, so the "14 kPa capillary clamp" is really about 0.1 kPa. Gravity head on a vertical phone is 19× larger than that and drains the oil downward. | Meniscus γ/r = 0.021/0.25 mm = 84–98 Pa; ρgh over 150 mm = 1.57 kPa. Lift scales as Dκ²/p, so it becomes 8.7 µm (5% set) and 35 µm (10%), against 52 and 209 nm claimed.
DEFECT | MAJOR | Even with an ideal clamp, the 52 nm figure treats the 50 µm and 30 µm glass sheets as rigid. With all layers bending together, the panel's set gives a micron-scale crease. | Stiffness ratio 0.0108: depth 2.6–5.2 µm and slope 0.95–1.9 mrad for 5–10% set.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The stack has no stated normal attachment to the frame. Supported only at its edges, it would sag millimetres face-down. The open oil edge lets in dust larger than the 3 µm films and lets in water, and both make visible spots. | Sag of an edge-supported 140 mm strip under 1 g is about 12 mm. Water/oil index step is 1.33/1.49, giving about 0.3% reflectance per interface.

Oppo states that recovery fades with use. "Within a minute" also needs about 80–240× acceleration, which is the 60 °C case, not 45 °C. | WLF gives 13.5–21.6× at 45 °C; 1–3 h of recovery divided by 45 s is 80–240×.
DEFECT | MAJOR | It does nothing for the only remaining set source in Ideas 1, 4 and 5, which is the glassy PI panel (the text concedes this). | The text itself.
DEFECT | MINOR | The energy is underestimated, and touch electrodes can't double as a continuous heater (touch would be unavailable while heating). The cross-references are wrong: the tension is in Idea 3, not Idea 2, and there are seven ideas, not four. | Heat capacity 34 J plus convection 77 J gives ≥111 J (0.13% of the battery), against 70 J claimed.

IDEA 7: Glass-carried thermally scheduled tension
DEFECT | MAJOR | Putting tension into the glass alone doesn't take the OCA out of the load path. Shear lag transfers the glass strain to the panel through the OCA, so the kill test comes back in cold conditions. | 70 MPa gives 0.097% strain over a 70 mm half-length, which is 68 µm of slip, or about 270% shear in 25 µm of OCA at −20 °C (120% at 25 °C).
DEFECT | MAJOR | The hot end depends on a 5 µm stretch, but glass length, clamp position and hinge tolerances are each tens of µm, and the 2:1 lever doubles any play. Without per-unit trimming, tension goes slack exactly when the polymers creep fastest. | 5 µm against Oppo's 50 µm best hinge variance.
DEFECT | MAJOR | The value depends on a ≤10 µm starting dent, which no measured shipping phone has. | Duo is 48 µm new; the N6 has no published absolute depth.
DEFECT | MINOR | The reaction path has to be much stiffer than the glass, which is hard to achieve through hinge pins with clearance. The ≤0.02° target has the same visibility gap as above. | Glass axial stiffness = 72 GPa × 50 µm × 0.15 m / 0.139 m = 3.9 N/µm.
SURVIVES | 1.62 µm/K, 239 MPa for 50 µm, 1.8 kN, 70 MPa, 522 N, 139 mm implied length, toggle work 35 mJ (0.4 N·m over 5°), clamp bond length 2–3.5 mm.
