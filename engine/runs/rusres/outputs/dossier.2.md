=== EVIDENCE ===
FACT 1: Yamal LNG final investment decision (FID) was taken in December 2013. The project cost was about $26.9–27bn. That covers the South Tambey field, the LNG plant (three 5.5 mtpa trains plus a small fourth train) and the Sabetta port. | HARD | https://www.nsenergybusiness.com/projects/yamal-lng-project
FACT 2: Yamal LNG train 1 loaded its first cargo in December 2017, about 4 years after FID. Train 2 started in July 2018 and train 3 in November 2018. Novatek said train 2 came six months early and train 3 more than a year ahead of the original schedule. Reaching 16.5 mtpa took about 5 years from FID. | HARD | https://www.lngindustry.com/liquefaction/22112018/yamal-lng-commences-third-lng-train-production/
FACT 3: Derived by me, not sourced: Yamal LNG cost about $1.6bn per mtpa, using $27bn over 16.5 mtpa. The $27bn includes the field, port and ships, so it overstates plant-only cost. | SOFT | https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/yamal-liquefied-natural-gas-project-russia-2018-09-13
FACT 4: Yamal LNG's fourth train is a 0.9 mtpa unit on Novatek's own "Arctic Cascade" liquefaction technology. It was due around 2019–2020. | SOFT | https://www.lngindustry.com/liquid-natural-gas/09082018/yamal-lng-dispatches-first-cargo-from-second-train-in-record-time/
FACT 5: Yamal LNG construction peaked at more than 30,000 people on site. | SOFT | https://totalenergies.com/node/8864
FACT 6: Arctic LNG 2 FID was in 2019. It was planned as three 6.6 mtpa trains (19.8 mtpa) on concrete gravity-based structures. Planned start dates were 2023, 2024 and 2025–26. The planned cost was $21.3bn, and TotalEnergies targeted 20–30% lower cost per tonne than Yamal. | HARD | https://www.ogj.com/pipelines-transportation/lng/article/14039323/arctic-lng-2-development-reaches-fid
FACT 7: Derived by me: Arctic LNG 2 planned cost was about $1.08bn per mtpa ($21.3bn over 19.8 mtpa). | SOFT | https://www.nsenergybusiness.com/news/novatek-arctic-lng-2-project-fid/
FACT 8: At SPIEF 2023, Mikhelson said Arctic LNG 2 capex would exceed $22bn, because the switch to electric drives needs its own power stations. The Technip estimate for the foreign-scope EPC part was about $7.6bn and the Russian part about $3.6bn. TotalEnergies exited after investing about $2.5bn. (Press secondary source.) | SOFT | https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2023/12/25/factbox-kak-novatek-stroit-arktik-spg-2-a117123
FACT 9: Arctic LNG 2 was added to the US SDN list in November 2023. Train 1 was paused for winter in 2024. By May 2025 it was idle with full tanks waiting for tankers, and train 2 lacked power capacity. Train 1 therefore ran well below design from 2023 to 2025 against a plan of full output at about 2.5 years after FID. | SOFT | https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2025/05/23/proizvodstvo-na-pervoy-ocheredi-arktik-spg-2-priostanovleno-istochnik-a164165
FACT 10: Vostok Oil: the deputy energy minister put total investment at about 10 trillion roubles (about $157bn per Reuters). Sechin later said 4 trillion roubles had been invested. The 790 km Vankor–Payakha–Bukhta Sever pipeline is rated up to 100 mtpa, and the terminal cost 117bn roubles. Exports of 30 mtpa are expected in H2 2027. The Payakha project was first announced for 2024, so the first-oil lag is at least 3 years. | SOFT | https://en.highnorthnews.com/home/russias-rosneft-led-arctic-oil-project-to-cost-10-trillion-roubles/106848
FACT 11: Vostok Oil's first oil reached the Bukhta Sever pipeline in September 2026. Sechin said 4 trillion roubles had been invested so far and that 30 mtpa shipments start in H2 of next year. (Press, dated via URL.) | SOFT | https://investfuture.ru/articles/putin-soobschil-o-nachale-prokachki-nefti-po-nefteprovodu-proekta-vostok-oyl-k-terminalu-bukhta-sever-37845094
FACT 12: Vankor: Vankorneft was set up in 2004 and commercial production began 21 Sept 2009, about 5 years later, in an area with no infrastructure. Output peaked at about 22 mt in 2016. The four-field group had fallen to about 14.8 mt by 2023. | HARD | https://rosneft.com/press/today/item/175201
FACT 13: Vankor's profitability norm was reported at 17% by a government official, which is not a cost figure. No lifting cost per barrel was found. | SOFT | https://www.bfm.ru/news/95791
FACT 14: Novoportovskoye: first oil came in August 2012 from a test well. Commercial operation was planned for 2015. The licence was extended to 2150. Reserves are 220 mt of recoverable oil and 260 bcm of gas. Gazprom Neft put more than 150bn roubles into the Gas of Yamal pipeline alone (115.5 km, 58.4 km underwater, 20 bcm/y). | SOFT | https://neftegaz.ru/news/view/110390/
FACT 15: Nornickel Sulphur Programme: announced in 2018 at $2.5bn with a 75% SO2 cut by 2023. The cost rose to $3.5bn in 2019 with the deadline moved to end-2025. It was later reported at up to $4.3bn, with the target date moved out two years because foreign contractors withdrew. Cost growth was 40–70% and the delay at least 2 years. | HARD | https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2023/07/27/iz-za-otkaza-podryadchikov-nornikel-buksuet-s-poslednim-etapom-sernoy-programmy-nadeetsya-na-reshenie-do-kontsa-23g-a50319
FACT 16: The first stage of the Sulphur Programme at the Nadezhda smelter (gas capture, acid plant, gypsum and sulphur output) started in October 2023 and cost about 180bn roubles (about $2bn). | HARD | https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2023/10/25/nornikel-obyavil-o-puske-flagmanskogo-proekta-snizheniya-vybrosov-dioksida-sery-v-norilske-a111115
FACT 17: Talnakh concentrator modernisation (done 2017) cost 47bn roubles and raised capacity 34%, from 7.6 to 10.2 mtpa. That is about 18 roubles per tonne of added annual capacity, derived by me as about 1.8bn roubles per extra mtpa. | HARD | https://www.miningweekly.com/article/nornickel-completes-upgrade-of-arctic-based-concentrator-2017-06-07
FACT 18: Talnakh concentrator No. 3 (third start-up complex) was planned for imported equipment and is now being built wholly on domestic equipment, announced March 2026. In 2023, 90% of the steel frame was erected at the ore-preparation and ore-feed facilities. No capex or date was found. | SOFT | https://www1.ru/news/2026/03/06/tretii-puskovoi-kompleks-talnaxskoi-obogatitelnoi-fabriki-v-norilske-postroiat-polnostiu-na-otecestvennom-oborudovanii.amp.html
FACT 19: Nornickel 2023: cash operating costs were $5.3bn, down 19% mainly from the weaker rouble. Capex fell 29% to about $3bn, partly because foreign suppliers' self-sanctions forced projects to be rescheduled. Revenue was $14.4bn and EBITDA margin 48%. No cost per tonne of Ni-eq was found. | HARD | https://www.mining.com/web/nornickels-2023-profit-slumps-51/
FACT 20: Nornickel cash operating costs were $5.1bn in 2024, down 3% on 2023. 2022 was $6.54bn. | HARD | https://nornik-upload.storage.yandexcloud.net/iblock/100/k049676q8k1b5prvttswpaz2hqkd7mx1/IFRS_review_2024_eng_full.pdf
FACT 21: Nornickel lost about half of its spare-parts and equipment suppliers after 2022 (June 2022 report). | SOFT | https://gmk.center/news/nornikel-poteryal-50-postavshhikov-zapchastej-i-oborudovaniya/
FACT 22: Nornickel expected lower metal output in 2023 because of a temporary dip in ore mining during the switch to new mining equipment. | SOFT | https://www.interfax.ru/business/945385
FACT 23: Polyus total cash cost (TCC) was $389/oz in 2023, down 25% on 2022, from the weaker rouble, higher grades and an antimony by-product credit. 2024 guidance was $450–500/oz. This is TCC, not AISC. | HARD | https://www.boerse-frankfurt.de/news/ec9de67f-fb87-4f1c-b6e1-06c2abc33888
FACT 24: Natalka TCC was $490/oz in 2023, up 11%, from lower grades and the mineral extraction tax rate rising from 1.2% to 2.4%. In H2 2023 it was $452/oz, helped by better recovery from favourable ore mineralogy. A flotation circuit was planned for 2025. | HARD | https://financialfilings.com/filings/polus/earnings-release/2021/1393877/
FACT 25: Polyus group gold recovery was 81.8% in 2022 and 82.7% in 2021. | HARD | https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/PLZL/operating-results-for-2h-2022-and-full-year-2022-u17bfwzlh9j2zyk.html
FACT 26: Polyus per-mine TCC in H1 2022: Natalka $382/oz, Olimpiada $433, Blagodatnoye $405, Verninskoye $404, Kuranakh $645/oz. | HARD | https://shareprices.com/rns/financial-results-for-the-first-half-of-2022-vp5l7a9t7qk752u/
FACT 27: Natalka's Q3 2018 AISC was $1,143/oz, against Olimpiada at $395/oz. The gap shows the large share of capex and stripping in a ramping mine. | HARD | https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/60GT/3rd-quarter-results-9i5cbv9d1v46o4w.html
FACT 28: Kupol/Dvoinoye (Kinross segment): production cost of sales was $304.5m in 2020 and $314.1m in 2019. Metal sales were $904.6m in 2020. Dvoinoye ore processed was 326 kt in 2020 against 435 kt in 2019. Dividing by gold-equivalent ounces gives the cost per ounce, but the ounce count was not in the excerpts. | HARD | https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701818/000110465921046344/tm2038115d3_ex99-1.htm
FACT 29: Udokan first stage (BMC/Udokan Copper): 15 mtpa ore and up to 150 kt/y Cu. Production of concentrate began 11 Sept 2023. A hydrometallurgical plant was due in Q2 2024. Cathode copper started 2 Sept 2026 after a fire delay. The 2018 plan was first production in 2021, and 2008 plans had construction starting in 2010 and design capacity in 2016. | HARD | https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2026/09/02/udokan-nachal-proizvodstvo-katodnoy-medi-posle-zaderzhki-iz-za-pozhara-a205107
FACT 30: Udokan capex: remaining first-stage capex was about $1.35bn ex-VAT in 2018, with $1.25bn to be raised. The project was asking for tax relief in 2023 because its financial model had worsened. The US sanctioned the operator on 12 Apr 2023. Construction began in 2019, so concentrate came about 4.5 years later. | SOFT | https://fomag.ru/news-streem/gigantskiy_mednyy_proekt_udokan_khochet_privlech_1_25_mlrd_do_nachala_19g
FACT 31: Baimskaya (KAZ Minerals 2020): capex rose from $5.5bn to about $8bn. The causes were a revised tailings facility, about a one-year delay, and about $600m for a port and about 200 km of road. Target was 70 mtpa ore and first production by end-2027. Roughly $114 of capex per tonne of annual ore throughput, derived by me. | HARD | https://www.miningweekly.com/article/russia-infrastructure-plans-raise-risk-for-baimskaya-copper-project-kaz-2020-11-18
FACT 32: Elga rail link: the 321 km Elga–Ulak line to the BAM opened in 2011. Mechel put its cost at up to $1.3bn, and an expert estimated $2–3bn. In 2014 the Russian Railways head cited about 70bn roubles incl. VAT for the 320 km. Capacity was expanded to 12 mtpa. (Press.) | SOFT | https://www.eastrussia.ru/news/rzhd_ne_vykupyat_u_mechela_zheleznuyu_dorogu_k_elginskomu_mestorozhdeniyu/
FACT 33: Elga output: 4.3 mt in 2019 and 5.83 mt in Jan–Nov 2020, against a 30 mt run-of-mine design (about 23 mt product) set in 2008. 26 mt were mined in 2023, up 30% from 20 mt in 2022. Reaching design output took about 12 years from the 2011 rail opening. Mechel sold 51% for 89bn roubles (about $1.17bn). | HARD | https://www.interfax.ru/business/941696
FACT 34: Elga: 220bn roubles were invested in 2020–2023. Seven concentrators were in service and 4 more were due in 2024, taking concentrate to 40 mtpa. Stage 2 targets 45 mtpa. | SOFT | https://neftegaz.ru/news/coal/805300-elga-uvelichila-v-5-raz-investprogrammu-i-dovela-uroven-dobychi-do-23-mln-t-god-uglya/
FACT 35: Alrosa's Udachny underground mine started in 2014 and reached design capacity in 2021, about 7 years later. It gave 4.4 Mct (14% of Alrosa's 32.4 Mct) in 2021. Construction cost about $1.4bn from preparatory work to full capacity, about $320 per annual carat, derived by me. | SOFT | https://www.mining-technology.com/news/newsalrosa-turns-udachnaya-diamond-mine-russia-underground-operation-4634056/
FACT 36: Alrosa's Udachnaya deep-horizons project (to −780 m) costs about 20bn roubles. It gives 4.1–4.4 Mt ore a year to 2055, about 6bn roubles a year profit, and construction runs to 2040. | SOFT | https://rapaport.com/news/alrosas-mine-life-extension-to-add-1-2b-to-its-coffers/
FACT 37: Alrosa's Mir mine flooded and stopped in 2017. Restoration was priced at 73bn roubles, with 6–8 years of construction, so a restart is unlikely before about 2030. Reserves are 68.6 Mct. | HARD | https://www.interfax.ru/russia/664006
FACT 38: Alrosa produced 34.6 Mct in 2023. Sales were 322.6bn roubles and net profit 85.2bn roubles. Cost of sales in 2024 was 157.5bn roubles, down 11%. Dividing by 2024 carats would give cost per carat, but I did not find the 2024 carat count. | HARD | https://www.idexonline.com/FullArticle?Id=49146
FACT 39: Tomtor: Rosneft took full control of the operator in May 2025 after Polymetal left in 2024. Pilot mining is expected in 2027. Putin criticised the owners for the delay in Nov 2024. No capex was found. | SOFT | https://www.spglobal.com/commodity-insights/en/news-research/latest-news/metals/052125-russias-oil-major-rosneft-takes-over-tomtor-rare-metals-deposit-in-the-far-east
FACT 40: Statutory expert review time: up to 30 working days per a blog source (secondary). The Arctic Zone has special rules for project documentation. Total design-and-review time for Arctic mines was not found. | SOFT | https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/273235/78689
FACT 41: Staffing: Norilsk contractor ads show rotation 60/30 (6/1, 10 h shifts, 80% northern premium) and 90/30. One construction foreman offer is 210,000 roubles net for 60 shifts at 6/1 and 12 h. These are job ads, not statistics. | SOFT | https://hh.ru/vacancy/130997021
FACT 42: Novatek Arctic LNG 2 owners: Novatek 60%, TotalEnergies, CNPC, CNOOC and Mitsui/JOGMEC 10% each. Saipem and partners had a 2.2bn euro construction contract. | SOFT | https://novayagazeta.eu/articles/2025/02/07/blizkaia-putinu-kompaniia-cherez-posrednikov-naniala-amerikanskikh-lobbistov-s-tseliu-sniat-sanktsii-s-proekta-po-proizvodstvu-szhizhennogo-gaza-arktik-spg-2-news
