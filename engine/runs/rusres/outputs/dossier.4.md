FACT 4: CHNL transit data for 2025: 103 transits by 88 vessels (52 east, 51 west), about 3.2 Mt of cargo, +6.2% in voyages and +3.2% in cargo. Season ran 30 Jun–17 Nov (about 4.5 months) in poor ice.
