I've finished the searches (about 28) and am now writing up the evidence. Many results were thin or dated, so the GAPS section lists what is still missing.

=== EVIDENCE ===
FACT 1: Nornickel Norilsk Division proven+probable ore reserves at 1 Jan 2024 were 1,203 Mt (709 Mt proven, 494 Mt probable) at about 0.71% Ni, 1.31% Cu, 4.52 g/t 6PGM. The Talnakh ore field holds 676 Mt proven (about 0.67% Ni, 1.33% Cu, 4.25 g/t 6PGM) and 389 Mt probable (about 0.94% Ni, 1.64% Cu, 5.17 g/t 6PGM). The estimates were done by SRK in 2021. The search tool said the column headers were garbled, so the Ni and Cu grade reading is an interpretation. | HARD | https://ar2023.nornickel.ru/en/business-overview.1.html
FACT 2: Norilsk-1 disseminated ore: 32 Mt proven at about 0.25% Ni, 0.33% Cu, 4.56 g/t 6PGM (1 Jan 2024). Mining is in the northern part of the deposit. | HARD | https://ar2023.nornickel.ru/en/business-overview.1.html
FACT 3: Norilsk Division reserves were about 1,058 Mt at 1 Jan 2023, against 1,203 Mt a year later. The 2023 decline was attributed to changed economic parameters and mineral extraction tax (MET) assumptions. | SOFT | https://ar2022.nornickel.ru/en/business-overview/mineral-base/mineral-resources.html
FACT 4: The Talnakh cluster was re-estimated with 3D modelling, adding 232 Mt of mineral resources. Group PGM reserves moved from about 112 Moz (2019) to 110 Moz (2020) to 158 Moz (2021). | SOFT | https://ar2021.nornickel.ru/pdf/ar/en/business-overview_mineral-base_existing-deposits.pdf
FACT 5: In the 2018 annual report, Taimyrsky was listed at 139 Mt of reserves (Ni 1.2%, Cu 1.9%, PGM 4.5 g/t) and Oktyabrsky at 219 Mt (Ni 0.7%, Cu 2.1%, PGM 5.9 g/t). These are historical figures. | SOFT | https://ar2018.nornickel.ru/en/business-overview.1.html
FACT 6: Oktyabrsky deposit: the programme to open the rich ores is at depths over 1.7 km. The tuff-lava sequence is 1,200 m thick, and Quaternary cover in valleys is 120–200 m. | SOFT | https://misis.ru/news/6595/
FACT 7: Maslovskoye (Nornickel licence since the 2015 discovery) has B+C1+C2 reserves of 206.8 Mt. They contain about 33,087 koz Pd, 13,040 koz Pt, 711 kt Ni, 1,098 kt Cu, 26 kt Co and 1,268 koz Au. Grades are 5.0 g/t Pd, 2.0 g/t Pt, 0.3% Ni and 0.5% Cu, so it is a low-grade, PGM-rich ore. The State Commission on Mineral Reserves (GKZ) approved it on 12 Oct 2018 (Minutes 5561). | HARD | https://ar2021.nornickel.ru/pdf/ar/en/business-overview_mineral-base_growth-projects.pdf
FACT 8: Chernogorskoye was held by Russian Platinum. A 50/50 joint venture with Nornickel was announced in Feb 2018 to cover the southern Norilsk-1 field and Chernogorskoye. State-balance reserves of Maslovskoye, southern Norilsk-1 and Chernogorskoye together were 3.7 kt PGM, 1.9 Mt Ni and 2.7 Mt Cu. Completion of the JV is unconfirmed. | SOFT | https://ar2018.nornickel.ru/en/business-overview.1.html
FACT 9: Nornickel drilled five 200 m thermometric boreholes in 2022 (Norilsk, Talnakh, Oganer, Kayerkan, Dudinka) to forecast permafrost change to 2050. Site permafrost thickness figures were not found. | HARD | https://www.comnews.ru/digital-economy/content/226271/2023-05-22/2023-w21/nornikel-razvivaet-arktike-set-skvazhin-dlya-nablyudeniya-za-vechnoy-merzlotoy
FACT 10: Baimskaya (Peschanka) JORC resource: 9.9 Mt Cu at 0.39% and about 16.6 Moz Au at 0.21 g/t (M+I+Inf). The licence area is about 1,300 km². | HARD | https://www.kazminerals.com/media/22180/completion-of-baimskaya-feasibility-study-final-20210928.pdf
FACT 11: An earlier KAZ Minerals figure (2018–2019) was 9.5 Mt Cu at 0.43% and 16.5 Moz Au at 0.23 g/t. The pre-feasibility study (PFS) assumed 250 kt Cu and 400 koz Au a year for 10 years, 60 Mtpa through two concentrators, and a mine life of about 25 years. | HARD | https://www.kazminerals.com/kaz-minerals-acquires-baimskaya-copper-project/
FACT 12: Baimskaya capex estimates range from $7 bn (2018) to $5.5 bn (later). By the 2021 feasibility study, start-up was planned for 2027 at about 300 kt Cu and 490 koz Au a year in the first 10 years. In July 2023 GDK Baimskaya passed to Trianon, owned by KAZ Minerals' ultimate shareholders. VEB was reported to be preparing a RUB 1.1 trn financing. | SOFT | https://www.nsenergybusiness.com/news/veb-commits-13-4bn-to-develop-baimskaya-copper-project-in-russias-far-east/
FACT 13: Udokan has JORC resources of about 26.7 Mt contained Cu. Reported grade is 0.95–1.1% depending on source. One source gives 2.7 Bt at 0.95% (2013); another gives 1.5 Bt of reserves at 1.05% Cu. The sources conflict and the reporting date and category are unclear. | SOFT | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udokan_Copper
FACT 14: Old Russian balance figures for Udokan: 1,375.2 Mt ore, 19.95 Mt Cu at 1.56%, and silver at 9.6 g/t. Ore types in industrial-category reserves are 43% sulphide, 40% mixed and 17% oxidised. The deposit is in a permafrost zone and a seismic zone rated 10 points. | SOFT | https://www.gazeta.ru/business/2008/09/10/2835531.shtml
FACT 15: Udokan licence history: Mikhailovsky GOK (Metalloinvest) won it in 2008 for RUB 15 bn, and it was later reissued to Baikal Mining Company (Udokan Copper). The first-stage mill is 15 Mtpa of ore for up to 135 kt Cu a year; the planned second stage is up to 550 kt Cu in total. Licence expiry was not found. | SOFT | https://expert.ru/news/vladimir-putin-razreshil-sdelki-s-aktsiyami-udokanskoy-medi
FACT 16: Tomtor is a carbonatite massif in the Ujinsky province of the NE Siberian platform with a weathering crust up to 400 m thick. Rich ores form three stratiform bodies (Severny, Yuzhny, Buranny). The ores are very fine-grained with 50–60% slimes, so conventional beneficiation is not applicable. | SOFT | https://resar.elpub.ru/jour/article/download/100/66
FACT 17: Buranny rich ores average 4.5% Nb2O5, 10% REE2O3, 0.75% Y2O3 and 0.06% Sc2O3 in one academic paper. Another source gives 6.7% Nb and 12.5% REO. The two sources conflict. | SOFT | https://my.bsu.ru/content/pbl/articles/article_763.pdf
FACT 18: On 20 May 2025 Rosneft became sole owner of Vostok Engineering, which holds the Buranny licence at Tomtor. The licence is reported valid to 2034. The site is about 400 km from the Laptev Sea and the Lena, with no transport or power infrastructure. Official GKZ reserves in tonnes were not found. | SOFT | https://www1.ru/news/2025/05/21/unikalnaia-sdelka-rosneft-polucila-kontrol-nad-krupneisim-v-rossii-mestorozdeniem-redkozemelny.html

Olimpiada is 30.7 Moz, Blagodatnoye 11.2 Moz, Natalka 9.6 Moz, Kuranakh 7.1 Moz and Verninskoye 3.1 Moz. | HARD | https://www.polyus.com/en/operations/reserves_and_resources
FACT 20: Polyus reserves in earlier years: Natalka about 16 Moz (2017) and about 14 Moz (2021), so reserves are falling with depletion. Kuranakh was 6.4 Moz and Kuranakh alluvials 0.54 Moz in the 2021 update. | HARD | https://www.polyus.com/en/media/press-releases/ore-reserves-and-mineral-resources-update/

Dvoinoye underground mining ended in Nov 2020, and stockpiled ore goes through the Kupol mill. The last NI 43-101 report is dated 31 Mar 2015. | SOFT | https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701818/000110465922041156/kgc-20211231xex99d1.htm

The categories differ. Open-pit mining began in 2014. | SOFT | https://bigenc.ru/c/pavlik-mestorozhdenie-b97026

GAPS: Official GBZ and GKZ balance figures (A+B+C1/C2) were not reached for any deposit; they sit in the Rosnedra and MNR annual state reports and the FGBU "Rosgeolfond" balance. Missing in particular are Tomtor reserves in tonnes, Udokan 2024 reserves, the Kyuchus and Yakutia/Chukotka placer totals, Inaglinsky, Malaya Lemberova, Lemberova, Bovanenkovo, Yuzhno-Tambeyskoye, and Vankor-cluster reserves. Licence terms and expiries are mostly missing (found only for Utrenneye and Tomtor).

Site-level permafrost class and ground-ice content were not found for any deposit. This includes the edge-of-permafrost cases Arkhangelsk (Grib, Lomonosov), Elga, Udokan and Kupol. They would need overlays of deposit coordinates on the Melnikov Permafrost Institute and VSEGINGEO geocryological maps, or Nornickel, Alrosa and Kinross engineering reports.

The 2024–25 company statements were not reached: the Nornickel 1 Jan 2025 table, the Alrosa 2024 JORC, and Novatek and Gazprom Neft 2024 audits. Several per-pipe Alrosa and Kinross numbers are old (2012–2021) or poorly parsed, so they need checking against the originals.
