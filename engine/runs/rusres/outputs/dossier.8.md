Searches are done (about 27). I'm writing up the evidence now.

=== EVIDENCE ===
FACT 1: Yamal LNG (3 trains × 5.5 Mtpa, finished end-2018) was built from 142 modules and 365 pipe racks, over 480,000 t, fabricated at Asian yards from Nov 2014 to Aug 2017. A Yamal LNG director said modules cut the construction period by 1.5 years against conventional methods. | HARD | https://www.technipfmc.com/media/p51juylp/yamal_lng_case_study.pdf
FACT 2: Novatek's Arctic LNG 2 builds each liquefaction line on a concrete gravity-based structure (GBS) at Belokamenka, near Murmansk. The first line took 37 months. In June 2023 Novatek set a target of 24–28 months per line, and I found no evidence it was reached. | SOFT | https://fomag.ru/news-streem/novatek-khochet-sokratit-sroki-stroitelstva-spg-liniy-do-24-28-mesyatsev/
FACT 3: A 640,000 t GBS was towed about 2,000 km from Belokamenka to Utrenneye in about 21 days in July–Aug 2023. A second followed in July–Aug 2024. Each is designed for up to 6.6 Mtpa. | HARD | https://thebarentsobserver.com/en/arctic-lng/2024/07/silence-descends-belokamenka
FACT 4: The third GBS stayed at Belokamenka in 2024–25. The Belokamenka centre was reported nearly emptied of workers. | SOFT | https://www.thebarentsobserver.com/arctic-lng/doomed-arctic-lng-platform-arrives-in-gydan/113016
FACT 5: Murmansk LNG was planned as 3 lines × 6.8 Mtpa using Novatek's own "Arctic Mix" technology, with two lines in 2027 and the third in 2029. The August 2025 government plan moves completion from 2030 to 2032, with 20.4 Mtpa capacity. There is no investment decision. US sanctions (June 2024) are the main cause. The Volkhov–Murmansk 40 bcm/y gas pipeline is due in 2031 and Gazprom is to finance it. | SOFT | https://neftegaz.ru/news/transport-and-storage/897486-sdvig-na-2-goda-proekt-novateka-murmanskiy-spg-mozhet-byt-zapushchen-v-2032-g/
FACT 6: Arctic LNG 2 Train 1 started in late Dec 2023. Novatek and its foreign shareholders declared force majeure after US sanctions. The terminal at Utrenneye is about 70 km from Sabetta. | HARD | https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/7419035
FACT 7: In 2025 China (Beihai terminal) received 22 shipments from sanctioned Russian LNG projects, all but one from Arctic LNG 2. Shipments rebounded in spring and summer 2026, with a record 8 vessels in June 2026, helped by better NSR transit conditions and a larger tanker fleet. | SOFT | https://www.gasprocessingnews.com/news/2025/12/china-received-22-shipments-of-lng-from-sanctioned-russian-projects-in-2025/
FACT 8: NSR cargo in 2025 was 37.02 Mt, down 0.87 Mt (−2.3%) on 2024. LNG, oil and bulk fell. Ore concentrate, oil products and containers grew. Exports were 60% (22.2 Mt). This is a Gekon estimate, and the government target was above 40 Mt. | SOFT | https://finance.mail.ru/article/arktika-ne-derzhit-nagruzku-69197769/
FACT 9: Yamal's Arc7 carriers were designed for NSR to Asia in summer only, and for westbound (Europe) voyages in winter. A later design allows year-round operation. The NSR is usable for these ships mainly from July to late November. The Asian leg takes 19–21 days against 35 days via Suez. | SOFT | https://gcaptain.com/eu-imports-more-yamal-lng-as-russian-arctic-trade-tilts-toward-europe/
FACT 10: Rosatom's land-based RITM-200N SMR at Ust-Kuyga, Yakutia, is 55 MW, with a 60-year life and 5-year refuelling. The construction licence was issued in April 2023 and commissioning is targeted for 2028. Camps for 250 and 683 people and a 12 km road were built by 2024. A two-reactor variant is under discussion. No capex has been published. Rosatom claims it will halve regional electricity cost. | HARD (schedule, specs); SOFT (cost claim) | https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/preparatory-work-stepped-up-for-russia-s-first-lan
FACT 11: For the Baimsky mine (Baimskaya), Rosatom is to supply 4 floating power units (RITM-200S reactors, 3 working plus 1 reserve). The Chukotka governor said 2 units in 2028 and 2 more by 2030 (Aug 2025 report). Key equipment is installed at Baltiysky Zavod. | SOFT | https://www1.ru/news/2025/08/16/rosatom-postavit-4-energobloka-dlia-baimskoi-rudnoi-zony-na-cukotke.amp.html
FACT 12: The Baimsky mine's investment was reported at about $8 bn, with demand of about 251 MW. | SOFT | https://www.eastrussia.ru/news/investitsii-v-baimskiy-gok-sostavili-vosem-mlrd-dollarov/
FACT 13: Raglan (Glencore, Nunavik) runs two 3 MW Enercon E-82 turbines and about 3 MW of storage. These save about 4 million L of diesel a year. A 2021 federal grant of C$7.1M supports 2 more turbines (about 6.6 million L a year saved) and 4 MW more storage. Total site diesel use is about 50 million L a year. | HARD (4M L, multiple sources); SOFT (6.6M L plan) | https://canadianminingjournal.com/news/feds-announce-7-1m-for-new-wind-turbines-at-raglan-mine
FACT 14: A Dec 2025 WSP environmental assessment summary covers 36 MW of wind plus battery storage at Raglan. No commissioning date or capex was found. | SOFT | https://www.ree.environnement.gouv.qc.ca/dossiers/3215-10-018/3215-10-018-9.pdf
FACT 15: A 2025 simulation study for Raglan (12 added E-115 turbines plus a 20 MW/160 MWh redox-flow battery) gives up to 63.98% diesel reduction and 68,000 t CO2 a year saved. The levelised cost of storage is CAD 7,831/MWh, which the authors call a barrier. | SOFT | https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/13/7147
FACT 16: Diavik's 4-turbine wind farm (9.2 MW, 2012) supplies about 17 GWh a year, displacing about 4–5 million L of diesel (about 10% of fuel). A 3.5 MW solar plant (built Feb–Jul 2024) saves about 1 million L a year and supplies up to 25% of power during closure work to 2029. Diavik was expected to end operations in early 2026. | HARD (wind); SOFT (solar claim) | https://newsletter.en.creamermedia.com/article/diavik-diamond-mine-solar-farm-canada-update-2024-10-11
FACT 17: Red Dog (Alaska) ships concentrate in a fixed window. The 2024 season ran 12 Jul–26 Oct (1.4 Mt wet, 22 ships, from barges). The 2025 season ran 11 Jul–21 Oct (1.2 Mt wet, 22 ships). | HARD | https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886986/000088698626000004/teck-20251231xexx993mda.htm
FACT 18: Teck guided Red Dog zinc down to 375–415 kt in 2026 and 330–370 kt in 2027, because slippage on a fault in the Aqqaluk pit forced mining of lower-grade areas. | HARD | https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886986/000095014226000706/eh260751304_ex9901.pdf
FACT 19: Mary River (Baffinland) shipped about 6 Mt in 2024 on 70 ore carriers, within the approved 6 Mtpa cap. The season is limited by ice and a narwhal management plan. In 2022 the company did not break ice to start the season, which delayed and shortened it by 1–2 weeks. The 10% workforce cut in Oct 2024 followed weak iron ore prices. The Steensby rail (year-round shipping) construction was still unfunded. | HARD | https://baffinland.com/_resources/document_portal/2024-NIRB-Annual-Report_Popular-Summary_ENG.pdf
FACT 20: Willow (ConocoPhillips, North Slope): capex was raised to $8.5–9 bn (from $7–7.5 bn), with first oil in early 2029. It was about 50% complete at the Nov 2025 update. Peak output is about 180 kb/d and reserves about 600 Mbbl, and inflation added about $700M. | HARD | https://worldoil.com/news/2025/11/6/conocophillips-lifts-willow-project-cost-to-9-billion-first-oil-set-for-early-2029/
FACT 21: Trans-Alaska pipeline: more than 124,000 ammonia heat pipes were installed in the mid-1970s on the vertical support members (VSMs). Non-condensable gas degraded some pipes. CO2 recharge worked as a repair. At Lost Creek, 8 thermal VSMs were replaced and 60 freestanding thermosyphons added. | HARD | https://ascelibrary.com/doi/10.1061/9780784483589.031
FACT 22: A 2024 study of 14 embankments on the Qinghai–Tibet network (2003–2020) found thermosyphon cooling weakening under warming, with taliks re-forming where they had been removed. The cause is a falling air-temperature freezing index. | HARD | https://doaj.org/article/186a6868af9d417f9ad90f1cadfc80e3
FACT 23: A 2024 Qinghai–Tibet railway study found a targeted thermosyphon array produced a −2 °C cold core at foundation centre within one year. A 2026 coupled model found thermosyphons reduce long-term settlement by up to 50%, but cause pronounced horizontal thermal gradients and moisture redistribution. | SOFT | https://doaj.org/article/6d141bc47f4a4b1cb7a18549263d7ead
FACT 24: Alaska North Slope tundra travel season fell from about 200 days (1970s) to about 130 days recently. The ice-road season ranged 123–209 days in the first decade after 2003 and 161–200 days afterwards. The state moved to an operating standard of −5 °C or colder in the top 30 cm of soil (23 °F), with snow-packing and ice stockpiling. | HARD | https://alaskabeacon.com/2022/10/17/study-tracks-reductions-in-time-and-space-for-ice-roads-in-the-arctic-including-alaska/
FACT 25: Russia's state background permafrost monitoring regulation was approved by Government Decree No. 854 of 25.06.2024, built on the Roshydromet network, with open data. | HARD | https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_478925/b6b79f2951956643a056ebfb7293b61db9285994/
FACT 26: A source on the Roshydromet network (undated) reports 78 observation points in 12 regions. The plan was 140 points by 2025 (deputy minister Anoprienko). | SOFT | https://www.pnp.ru/social/do-2025-goda-v-arktike-postroyat-140-punktov-monitoringa-za-tayaniem-vechnoy-merzloty.html
FACT 27: Nornickel's geotechnical monitoring of buildings on permafrost ran in trial operation from 30 Dec 2021. It has five 200 m boreholes (Norilsk, Talnakh, Oganer, Kayerkan, Dudinka). Piles are checked by seismoacoustic and ultrasonic methods, with geodetic deformation surveys. | SOFT | https://neftegaz.ru/news/ecology/780567-nornikel-prodolzhaet-razvivat-sistemu-monitoringa-vechnoy-merzloty-v-arktike/
FACT 28: Sentinel-1 InSAR on Yamal (2017–18) measured seasonal surface change of 60–120 mm/yr, up to 180 mm/yr in active-layer zones. Subsidence was 3.9–4.3 mm per 100 thaw degree-days, matching in-situ data. Snow cover creates an early-thaw gap in the record. These are ground-surface values, not structural settlement. | HARD | https://piahs.copernicus.org/articles/382/183/2020/
FACT 29: Aitik (Boliden) trolley-assist: a 2018 pilot of 700 m with four Cat 795F trucks. The expansion cost SEK 300M ($31.2M), adding 3 km of line and 10 trucks. Boliden estimated diesel cut by 5,500 m³ a year and life-of-mine transport GHG down by nearly 15%. | HARD | https://www.e-mj.com/departments/operating-strategies/project-plans-underline-industry-shift-to-green-mining-initiatives/
FACT 30: Kiruna (LKAB) main level 1365 m cost SEK 12.4 bn and took about 4.5 years to build per E&MJ. It is run with driverless electric trains, and ore output was to rise from about 27 to 35 Mtpa. Granitor gives a 10-year build period for the level, and LKAB's own 2018 announcement says it was completed around 2017, so the build time is disputed. | SOFT | https://lkab.com/en/news/one-of-swedens-biggest-industrial-investments-completed/
FACT 31: Agnico Eagle Meliadine 2024: 378,886 oz gold (+4%), 1,966 kt milled at 6.22 g/t. Meadowbank complex 2024 payable production was 504,719 oz. Company all-in sustaining cost (AISC) was $1,239/oz. No mill recovery figure was found. | HARD | https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2809/000110465925013049/aem-20241231xex99d1.htm
FACT 32: Sensor-based sorting cases (vendor-reported): Renison tin cut unit cost per tonne of tin by about 10% (throughput +15–20%). San Rafael tin recovery rose from 90.5% to 92.5% with a 4-month payback, and 24% of feed is now low-grade ore. | SOFT | https://miningmagazine.com/processing/news/1378855/tomras-xrt-san-rafael
FACT 33: Dry-stacked or filtered tailings in the cold: freeze-thaw cycles alter density and permeability, and thaw rapidly reduces bearing capacity. Winter-frozen layers can leave man-made permafrost lenses that settle on thaw. Coupled freeze-thaw models are immature and long-term Arctic monitoring data is scarce. Keno Hill's dry stack reported the permafrost surface remained frozen. | SOFT | https://srk.com/en/publications/dry-stack-tailings-in-cold-regions
FACT 34: The Eagle Gold (Yukon) heap leach failed in June 2024. The independent review board found a steep section with a history of poor drainage failed, with owner and contractor "actions and inactions" contributing. Millions of tonnes of cyanide-laced ore were released. The sources do not mention permafrost as a cause. | HARD | https://yukon.ca/en/news/government-of-yukon-provides-update-on-heap-leach-failure-response-at-victoria-golds-eagle-gold-mine-dec-13
FACT 35: Microreactor costs have no built-unit data. Westinghouse expects about $0.25/kWh for the first units. DOE/INL puts the Alaska remote-application average at $0.54/kWh. An MIT-led study found diesel+wind, microreactor and other options all at about $0.42–0.45/kWh. The Bruce Power/Westinghouse study found up to 44% savings versus diesel. A Canadian roadmap found 24 mines needing about 600 MWe, mostly 5–20 MWe. | SOFT | https://miningnewsnorth.com/story/2022/05/06/in-depth/a-nuclear-option-for-mines-in-the-north/7354.html
FACT 36: Snøhvit Future (Hammerfest LNG electrification): cost estimate rose from NOK 13.2 bn (2022) to NOK 26.5 bn (2026 value), blamed on plant operations, weather and tunnel rock. Compression is planned for 2029 and electrification for 2030, with the project 60% complete. | HARD | https://www.equinor.com/news/20261007-updated-cost-estimate-snohvit-future
FACT 37: Tanbreez (Greenland) capex is reported inconsistently: $290M (Oct 2025) against $800M–1bn (Jan 2026). The feasibility study was due Q4 2025, and no opex was found. Amitsoq graphite capex was $131M in the 2023 PEA and $321–340M in the 2024 feasibility study including a downstream plant. A 30-year exploitation licence was granted on 9 Dec 2025. | SOFT | https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/critical-metals-unveils-saudi-jv-100-tanbreez-offtake-and-800m1b-capex-outlook-2026-01-23/
FACT 38: Alrosa: the Mir mine flooded in Aug 2017, and restoring it was estimated at RUB 73 bn and 6–8 years. A new road surface for ore haulage at Udachny finished pilot trials. | SOFT | https://www.interfax.ru/russia/664006
FACT 39: A Russian robotic haul-truck pilot (Tsifra, Izykhsky open pit) reported productivity about 20% above human norms, and one operator running several trucks. This is a developer claim and not from an Arctic site. | SOFT | https://habr.com/ru/articles/930112
FACT 40: Nornickel was reported in Feb 2026 to be preparing a 700 t "Norda" roadheader to raise automation of ore mining in the Norilsk area. This is a single press source. | SOFT | https://overclockers.ru/world/lenta/17-02-2026

GAPS: No capex, opex or recovery figures were found for Raglan wind and storage, Meliadine or Amaruq (NI 43-101 reports), Aitik or Kiruna automation. These sit in the Glencore/Tarquti, Agnico and Boliden technical reports and annual reports. Russian pilots at Polyus (Natalka, Kupol), Nornickel (Norilsk autonomous haulage, wind or solar) and Alrosa (underground methods) returned nothing; look in the company annual and sustainability reports and in the journals Gornyi Zhurnal and Zoloto i Tekhnologii. Nothing was found on heap leach in deep cold, on Chinese modular LNG and microreactors, or on Arc7 year-round utilisation numbers and Rosatom icebreaker delivery schedules. Vendor and Russian-press claims dominate where hard data is missing.
