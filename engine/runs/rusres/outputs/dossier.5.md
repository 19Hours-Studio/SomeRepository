=== EVIDENCE ===
Note: search excerpts only, with no page fetches. Most items are press or vendor statements, so many are SOFT. Dates are given where the source gave them.

FACT 1: Arctic LNG 2 trains use licensed Linde MFC4 liquefaction (three cooling cycles, adapted for cold and the compact gravity-based structure). Novatek's own Arctic Cascade and Arctic Mix are not used there. The Linde licence was bought, but Linde has left Russia. | SOFT | https://finance.mail.ru/article/novatek-arkticheskie-problemy-60543843/
FACT 2: Arctic Cascade runs only on Yamal LNG Train 4, at 0.9 Mt/y. Arctic Cascade is planned for Ob LNG and Arctic Mix for Murmansk LNG. Both are Novatek-patented. | HARD | https://neftegaz.ru/news/standarts/787761-novatek-zakazal-niokry-po-lokalizatsii-spg-tekhnologii-linde-na-desyatki-milliardov-rubley/
FACT 3: Yamal LNG Train 4 was described as the first LNG line with mainly Russian equipment. Reported suppliers: Kazanskompressormash (gas compressor unit), Kriogenmash (cryogenic heat exchanger), Atomenergomash (vaporisers and pumps), Roscosmos-linked firms (expander). Low-temperature alloy piping had to be replaced; delay damage was estimated at RUB 0.9bn. | SOFT (2018–19 press) | https://abnews.ru/2018/11/22/tretya-liniya-yamal-spg
FACT 4: Train 4 unit capex was put at USD 450–500 per t/y, against about USD 1,100 per t/y on Trains 1–3. | SOFT | https://abnews.ru/2018/11/22/tretya-liniya-yamal-spg
FACT 5: Novatek says Arctic Cascade uses about 220 kW per tonne, and that Air Products technology needs about 20% more energy. This is a company claim. | SOFT | https://kmu.itmo.ru/file/download/application/35164
FACT 6: Yamal LNG Trains 1–3 use APCI-C3MR with Nuovo Pignone (Baker Hughes) turbo-compressors, three drives per train. That is a Western spares dependence for the largest operating Russian LNG plant. | SOFT | https://kmu.itmo.ru/file/download/application/35164
FACT 7: Baker Hughes refused to deliver the contracted LM9000 turbines. Novatek received only 4 of the 20 gas turbines needed for the three Arctic LNG 2 trains. | SOFT | https://highnorthnews.com/en/china-supply-key-turbines-novateks-arctic-lng-2
FACT 8: Project documents put Arctic LNG 2 power at 20 gas turbines providing 482 MW. Four Wison power modules were built at Zhoushan and the last two shipped to Russia. The US sanctioned the Zhoushan yard in January 2025. | SOFT | https://gcaptain.com/exclusive-china-sends-key-arctic-lng-2-modules-to-russia-to-complete-train-2/
FACT 9: Wison New Energies stopped all Russian work in June 2024 (Reuters via BOE Report). This removed a Chinese equipment channel because of secondary-sanction risk. | HARD | https://boereport.com/2024/06/21/chinas-wison-new-energies-to-quit-russian-projects-in-blow-to-arctic-lng-2/amp/
FACT 10: Novatek dropped the Harbin Guanghan turbines because of delivery-timing risk. It ordered three GTE-170 from Power Machines for Train 2 power. Analysts note the GTE-170 has lower manoeuvrability in an isolated grid. | SOFT | https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2023/05/16/novatek-nashel-oborudovanie-dlya-arktik-spg-2-u-kitayskikh-harbin-guanghan-gas-turbine-i-wison-a42995
FACT 11: GTE-170 (Power Machines, built at LMZ): lead prototype assembled December 2022. Total investment exceeds RUB 15bn, of which RUB 4.8bn is state subsidy. The development programme started in 2018. By January 2026 the second GTE-170.1 was being prepared for shipment to Artyomovskaya TPP-2 (Far East), so serial output is at the level of units. | HARD | https://power-m.ru/en/press-center/news/power-machines-completes-the-assembly-of-the-lead-prototype-of-the-russian-high-power-gas-turbine-gt/ ; https://www1.ru/en/news/2026/01/17/zamena-siemens-silovye-masiny-gotoviat-k-otgruzke-vtoruiu-gazovuiu-turbinu-gte-1701-dlia-artemovskoi-tec-2.amp.html
FACT 12: Arctic LNG 2 Train 1 started December 2023 and Train 2 in May 2025. Forbes estimates actual output at about 3.6–4.4 Mt/y against 6.6 Mt/y per train design. Train 3 has slipped to about 2028. Sanctions and a shortage of ice-class vessels are given as the constraints. | SOFT | https://investfuture.ru/articles/forbes-arktik-spg-2-naraschivaet-otgruzki-vopreki-sanktsiyam-stavka-na-kitay-i-flot-arc7
FACT 13: Murmansk LNG (3 trains, over 20 Mt/y, 1,400 km pipeline from southern Gydan) is planned on own Arctic Mix technology. Novatek claims it will be cheaper than Yamal and Gydan LNG because it has no field-infrastructure cost. No unit capex is published. | SOFT | https://www.argusmedia.com/ru/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2461517-novatek-planiruet-vypuskat-spg-v-murmanske-po-sobstvennoy-tehnologii
FACT 14: Nornickel and BelAZ signed a joint-venture agreement on 7 September 2026 at Zhodino. It covers underground mining equipment production, repair and service. Production is planned at Sosnovoborsk, Krasnoyarsk Krai, at about 150 machines/y (a single source for the capacity). | SOFT | https://myfin.by/article/belarus/nornikel-i-belaz-postroat-zavod-gornoj-tehniki-v-sibiri-45134
FACT 15: BelAZ-75855 (45 t underground truck) uses a Tutaevsky Motor Plant 8523.10-01 engine (KAMAZ subsidiary; 17.2 L, 405 kW/550 hp). In October 2026 it was on factory-ground tests. It and the BelAZ-79550 loader are to go to Nornickel mines for pilot operation. BelAZ had never made underground equipment before. | SOFT | https://www1.ru/en/news/2026/10/08/550-l-s-pod-zemlei-tutaevskii-zavod-osnastil-45-tonnyi-belaz-dlia-norilska.html
FACT 16: Nornickel's earlier Polar Division fleet was supplied by Epiroc, Caterpillar, Sandvik and Normet. A pre-2022 purchase included 20 BelAZ-7555B, 7 Liebherr PR 764 and 2 Caterpillar 990 loaders (about USD 20m). So the installed base is Western and spares are the issue. | SOFT | https://www.e-mj.com/departments/suppliers-report/nornickel-upgrades-underground-mining-fleet/
FACT 17: A 2026 article in Gornaya Promyshlennost (No. 4) states that supplies of Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hitachi, Liebherr and Volvo CE heavy mining equipment stopped in 2022–2025. The authors argue that replacement efficiency depends on ownership cost, productivity and payback, not on the domestic share. | SOFT | https://mining-media.ru/images/2026/4_2026/84-92.pdf
FACT 18: BelAZ's chief designer claims about 90% of the Russian mining haul-truck market. BelAZ is localising on allied-country components: a Pinsk JV makes hydraulic pumps, and it works with the Yaroslavl, Tutaev and Ural engine plants. | SOFT | https://myfin.by/article/biznes/kak-belaz-stroit-budusee-souznymi-tehnologiami-i-alternativnoj-energetikoj-40790
FACT 19: Chinese excavators in Russia (Sany's own figures, Dec 2025): over 199 units of the 50 t class supplied by 2024 (Shantui 85, LiuGong 22, LGCE 20). Sany 120 t class rose from 1 unit in 2022 to 29 by 2024. Market demand: about 400–600 units/y for 50 t class and 80–100 units/y for 120 t class. LiuGong cites staff shortage and the unsolved problem of electric machinery for harsh climates. | SOFT (vendor) | https://ctt-expo.ru/tpost/vae6vcayi1-the-russian-mining-equipment-market-top
FACT 20: Heavy-bulldozer imports in H1 2024 exceeded all of 2023, with Chinese brands leading. The 33–90 t excavator segment fell 45%. A recycling fee (утильсбор) is flagged as an import barrier. Component quality is named as the main obstacle for Russian equipment. | SOFT | https://finance.rambler.ru/realty/53809906-spikery-onlayn-konferentsii-ekskavator-ru-sdelali-srez-rynka-gornodobyvayuschey-tehniki/
FACT 21: The Russian drilling-rig market in 2025: imports over 69% of the market. Domestic output fell 37.5% to RUB 19bn. Physical imports rose 3.7% to 2.4 thousand rigs. Local plants lost to Asian equipment (Tebiz, via RBC Marketing). | SOFT | https://marketing.rbc.ru/articles/16692/
FACT 22: Rosnedra head: domestic drilling equipment in exploration is over 90%, geophysical equipment about 50/50, and complex analytical instruments are replaced slowly. The main problem is Western software for 2D/3D seismic and 3D geological and mine-geological modelling. | SOFT | https://finance.mail.ru/article/glava-rosnedr-otsenil-uroven-nezavisimosti-ot-importa-v-geologii-rf-69203234/
FACT 23: Manturov: Russian equipment share in exploration and hydrocarbon production rose from 30% to 65% over ten years. The target is 90% technological sovereignty in oil and gas by 2030. The Minenergo head said the domestic share of mining-ore equipment exceeded 65% (July 2025), with no stated method. | SOFT | https://finance.mail.ru/article/denis-manturov-i-aleksandr-novak-proveli-zasedanie-koordinacionnogo-soveta-po-importozamesheniyu-68334417/
FACT 24: Minpromtorg estimate: foreign equipment is 25–30% of oil production overall but 100% on the offshore shelf. For new equipment the Russian share is below 20%, and Soviet-era rigs distort the statistics. | SOFT | https://neftegaz.ru/analisis/view/7889
FACT 25: Russian geology software: Rostec's RT-Engineering "Geomix" (6 modules: geology, surveying, planning, drill-and-blast, muck-pile modelling, geostructure) is positioned as a Micromine replacement. In October 2026 free academic licences went to over 30 universities. Eight Russian and three foreign firms bought two state-funded Industrial Competence Centre "Subsoil Use/Exploration" IT solutions. Commercial deployment figures at Nornickel or Polyus were not found. | SOFT | https://www.akm.ru/press/rostekh_zamestil_importnoe_po_dlya_studentov_gorno_geologicheskikh_spetsialnostey/ ; https://www.comnews.ru/content/233139/2024-05-15/2024-w20/1008/geologicheskie-it-proekty-industrialnogo-centra-kompetenciy-pokazali-okupaemost
FACT 26: Autonomous haulage in Russia: Zyfra tested BelAZ-7513R (130 t) autonomous trucks in Khakassia. Its own figures are 2,500 h of tests and 500,000 t of ore in 2.5 months. KAMAZ and Zyfra agreed to integrate a domestic dispatch system in 2022. BelAZ claims 20% transport-efficiency gain from remote and autonomous operation. No Arctic deployment count was found. | SOFT | https://www.zyfra.com/industries/robotics/ ; https://rostec.ru/en/media/news/kamaz-is-to-launch-the-first-russian-autonomous-mining-dump-trucks/
FACT 27: Russian autonomy developers (including Cognitive Pilot) must use hybrid navigation because satellite signals are lost in deep pits (KuzGTU preprint). Cognitive Pilot's Kirovets work is agricultural, not mining. | SOFT | https://science.kuzstu.ru/wp-content/Events/Conference/RM/2026/RM26/pages/Articles/10608.pdf
FACT 28: BelAZ says Alrosa-type deep diamond pits do not need drivers, and unmanned cabin-less trucks are technically simple. Customers still ask for a cab. BelAZ-7513R series is out and unmanned 90 t 7558R is in preparation. | SOFT | https://devby.io/news/samosval-na-million
FACT 29: Nornickel Group average headcount was 78,738 in 2024 (2023: 78,374; 2022: 80,562). Average monthly salary was RUB 207 thousand, 2.1 times the national average. Salaries rose 69% over 2019–2024, with a 10% rise in 2024. Turnover was 11.4% in 2023, and in 2024 only "in line with industry" is stated. 94.3% are covered by the collective agreement. | HARD | https://sr2024.nornickel.ru/en/about-norilsk-nickel/key-figures.html
FACT 30: Mining-engineer average pay rose from RUB 112 thousand (2023) to RUB 158 thousand (2025). A 2020–2024 study describes a critical shortage of qualified staff in the mineral sector, worsened by the pandemic, demographics and sanctions. | SOFT | https://journals.kuzstu.ru/article/4484.pdf
FACT 31: General labour market: one source forecasts an extra shortage of up to 300 thousand specialists. More than 70% of vacancies are in the Urals, Siberian and Far Eastern districts, which hold under 26% of the population. HeadHunter: active vacancies fell 31% and CVs rose 34% year-on-year in November 2025 (blog analytics). | SOFT | https://finance.rambler.ru/economics/51746546-rossiya-stolknulas-s-novym-kadrovym-defitsitom/
FACT 32: Northern pay rules: Labour Code art. 317 sets the percentage northern allowance (from 10% rising to 80–100% of earnings by region and length of service). In Chukotka and the Severo-Evensky district of Magadan Oblast it is paid at the 100% maximum. Rotation (вахта) workers get the allowance on general rules (art. 302). Regions are listed in Government Decree No. 1946 of 16.11.2021. Young workers on the Far North can get the maximum allowance without a service record. | HARD | https://www.consultant.ru/law/podborki/razmer_severnoj_nadbavki/ ; https://ppt.ru/news/podderzhka/molodezh-rabotayushchaya-na-kraynem-severe-smozhet-poluchit-maksimalnye-nadbavki
FACT 33: Sample rotation offer in Yakutia mining (single advertisement): 60-shift rotation at RUB 160,000 per month net. Alrosa offers employer-paid flights (own airline), baggage up to 23 kg, northern allowance from day one and a RUB 18,000 monthly meals payment. These are single postings, not statistics. | SOFT | https://hh.ru/vacancy/135933305
FACT 34: Magadan Oblast population fell by 1,339 in 2022 to 134,568 (1 January 2023): migration loss 805, natural loss 534. | HARD | https://27.rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/Предварительная оценка численности населения Магаданской области на начало 2023 года.pdf
FACT 35: Ground-cooling (thermosiphon) systems GET/VET: underground cooling pipes plus an aboveground heat exchanger, working without electricity. They work only in the cold season. A GET system has a condenser fin area of about 100 m², and is installed at the foundation stage (used at Chayanda). The market lacks a single approach to their use. No Fundamentstroyarkos price or capacity figure was found. | SOFT | https://vestnik.cstroy.ru/jour/article/download/558/443 ; https://neftegaz.ru/science/booty/711989-tekhnochayanda-tekhnologiya-termostabilizatsii-grunta/
FACT 36: US sanctioned Arctic LNG 2 (SDN list) in November 2023. Western parts and service channels were shut for the Arctic LNG 2 trains at that point, and Chinese suppliers' sanction risk followed (see FACTS 8–9). | HARD | https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2023/11/03/factbox-kak-novatek-stroit-arktik-spg-2-a112248
FACT 37: Parallel-import regime: it works only for brands and customs codes on the Minpromtorg list (Order No. 1532, 19.04.2022). Many brands are missing, so parts come through grey channels at a higher price. This is a general auto-parts finding, not specific to mining. | SOFT | https://www.zr.ru/content/articles/940529-ehti-zapchasti-nelzya-najti-v-ro/
FACT 38: Chinese-built 120 t electric mining truck (Xiangtan) is rated for −30 to +60 °C. No Chinese heavy-truck rating at −50/−60 °C was found. | SOFT | https://gmk.center/news/kitaj-vypustil-120-tonnyj-karernyj-samosval-na-elektroenergii/amp/

GAPS: (1) Cold-rating, uptime, failure and spares-lead-time data for Chinese or domestic mining fleets at −50 °C (SANY, XCMG, Liugong, Sinotruk, Kirovets, BelAZ) were not found. Look in Gornaya Promyshlennost, GIAB and Mining World Russia talks, and in the reports of Alrosa, Polyus and Nornickel. (2) Rosstat/Mintrud occupational shortage figures by region (Yakutia, Taimyr, Chukotka, YaNAO), rotation rosters, turnover, productivity per worker, and Alrosa/Polyus headcount were not retrieved. Look in Rosstat regional bulletins, the sustainability reports, and hh.ru regional analytics. (3) Chinese-language supplier specifications and export contracts, and GISP register entries, were not searched effectively. (4) The status of Atomenergomash and Kriogenmash cryogenic exchangers for Arctic Cascade at scale, Fundamentstroyarkos prices, K-MINE and Zyfra deployment counts, and Gydan GBS equipment status were not found.
