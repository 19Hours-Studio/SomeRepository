=== TRIAGE ===

IDEA 1 | I 5 | P 5 | DECIDES: series-built capex ($/t/y) at ≥90% availability | It copies the proven 0.9 Mtpa size, which avoids the scale-up failure, but the $450–500/t/y capex is a single unverified claim.

IDEA 2 | I 6 | P 5 | DECIDES: unit capex <$600/t/y after the piping fix | Sharing the base, port and berths addresses capital, the largest term. A 20–30% cost cut fits the 1.73x capital ceiling, but Arc7 tonnage still caps delivery.

IDEA 3 | I 3 | P 4 | DECIDES: differential settlement per interval vs 300 mm jack stroke | Ice-rich ground can settle by metres, and heavy plant and piping will not tolerate re-levelling. It suits only light structures.

IDEA 4 | I 4 | P 3 | DECIDES: firm spare MW at Chaun–Pevek after Bilibino closes | The node likely goes into deficit, not surplus. The schedule gain is real, but Archetype D has no room for 3x.

IDEA 5 | I 6 | P 4 | DECIDES: availability and capex of a 2 Mtpa train | It scales Train 4 by 2–3x on domestic drives, so it partly repeats the unproven scale-up. The Carnot gain is already in Train 4.

IDEA 6 | I 2 | P 7 | DECIDES: belt availability vs lump size and haul length | This is proven practice (Kiruna) and is mainly a spares hedge. The 3–6% gain is small.

IDEA 7 | I 3 | P 6 | DECIDES: measured summer heat flux under the plant (W/m²) | The physics is sound and the parasitic load is about 1%. It applies only to LNG sites and adds a single-point dependency on plant uptime.

IDEA 8 | I 1 | P 6 | DECIDES: movable overhaul hours vs cold-failure hours | Archetype C has no room for 3x, and the gain is 2–4%. Many overhauls are condition-driven and cannot be moved.

IDEA 9 | I 3 | P 3 | DECIDES: mass rejected at <3% metal loss | Disseminated sulphide gives weak XRT contrast, and sorting underground adds complexity. The diamond precedent does not transfer.

IDEA 10 | I 3 | P 7 | DECIDES: depth to competent rock ≤30 m | This answers the Ambarnaya failure directly with known mechanics. It is limited to rock sites and excludes Yamal.

IDEA 11 | I 5 | P 5 | DECIDES: capex/t·y and availability at −45 °C | The serial 16–25 MW pipeline turbines are real. Seven small trains lose economies of scale, and the idea duplicates IDEA 2.

IDEA 12 | I 5 | P 2 | DECIDES: firm MW available from the node (60–70 MW needed) | That much is not spare at Chaun–Bilibino, so the power assumption repeats the reason the KAZ plan slipped.

IDEA 13 | I 2 | P 4 | DECIDES: mass rejected vs gold lost in the reject | Rados sorters are domestic, but fine refractory gold sorts poorly. Archetype C has no room.

IDEA 14 | I 3 | P 6 | DECIDES: air freezing index vs heat input to the pile | It is valid at cold Norilsk and has a narrow scope. It extends infrastructure life rather than cutting cost.

IDEA 15 | I 4 | P 5 | DECIDES: Arc7 round-trip days Jan–Apr | It largely exists already (Saam FSU at Murmansk). Arctic LNG 2 went unsold because of sanctioned buyers, not ship-days. The cut is about 7% of delivered cost.

Priority for the expensive checks: IDEA 2 (merge with IDEAS 1 and 11), then IDEA 10, IDEA 7 and IDEA 15.
