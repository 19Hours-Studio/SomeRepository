These are single-source, partial checks. I computed the arithmetic myself in `calc.py` and `calc.out` in the working directory. The measured inputs came from search excerpts only, and I did not open any paper in full.

IDEA 1: f, the share of write tokens that are rewrites after expiry | required f ≈ 0.4 for the claimed ~15% saving (the idea's own model) | available f ≈ 0.25–0.45 if the session has exactly one idle expiry (my arithmetic on a 50-turn, 20k+2k/turn agent: expiry at turn 10, 25 or 40 gives 0.25, 0.37, 0.45). No measured distribution of idle gaps exists. I found only anecdotes of 7-minute and 13-hour gaps (towardsai and Medium posts; dirge-agent docs: reads refresh the 5-minute entry, so live loops stay warm). | MARGIN ≈ 0.9 for one expiry per session (range 0.6–1.1). The result is unmeasured, and f is about 0 for sessions with no gap over 5 minutes.

IDEA 2: hit-rate gain over best practice, which is already append-only | required: a gain of several points over the 94–98% per-turn hit rate the idea itself cites | available: about 0 points against an already append-only agent (FACT 6: an erratum costs 1.022x, so append-only is already at its floor; the Anthropic multipliers are exact). The gain exists only against clients that edit in place, and I found no data on how many do. | MARGIN ≈ 0 for append-only agents; unknown for in-place editors.

IDEA 3: share of total cost that comes from moving KV | required: at least ~25% of cost or latency, so the removal is worth a split-attention system | available: ≤ 25% of per-turn latency, and about 0% when transfer is overlapped layer by layer. My arithmetic for 70B, 100k context, 2k suffix, 8-way tensor parallel: 4.1 GB of KV per GPU takes 82 ms at 50 GB/s, against ~251 ms of suffix compute, so the unoverlapped share is 24.6%. Mooncake-style layerwise streaming (FACT 3) hides it. Traffic ratio 8n/L = 0.16, which is 6.25x, matches the idea. | MARGIN ≈ 1.0 at best, ~0 when overlapped. The traffic ratio is confirmed, but the cost it removes is small.

IDEA 4: gap recovered by partial recompute at 10–15% on long or multi-hop work (loss ≤3% means recovery ≥0.97) | required ≥ 0.97 | available 0.29. This is from InfoFlow KV (arXiv 2603.05353): LongBench v2 subset, Qwen3-14B, 15% recompute, full prefill 0.3833, no recompute 0.2111, CacheBlend 0.2611, so a 32% relative loss. It is one small 180-sample subset. FusionRAG and Decoupled Attention Fusion report gaps too (FACT 4), but I found no figures for them. | MARGIN ≈ 0.30

IDEA 5: share of the best-practice agent bill caused by in-place middle edits | required: ≥ ~10% of the bill, for a visible gain (the idea's 1.85x is a simulation with edits on 20% of turns) | available: about 0% for agents that already append. IDEA 6: fraction of the quality gap recovered by repair on multi-source queries | required ≥ 0.95 | available 0.5–0.67 for KVShareArena (arXiv 2609.10266, via a search summary of "half to two thirds"; that figure includes methods that train). CORVUS's 9–50% input reduction includes effects beyond deduplication. | MARGIN ≈ 0.9–1.0 (token-level duplicate share unmeasured)

IDEA 8: PCIe bandwidth to fetch top-K pages | required 8.7 GB/s for a 2k-token budget and 17.5 GB/s for 4k, per sequence (Llama-3-8B at 128 KiB/token, 30 ms/token, my arithmetic) | available ≈ 31.5 GB/s for PCIe (the figure NOSA and ShadowKV cite), ~50 GB/s PCIe5 practical, ~7 GB/s NVMe.
