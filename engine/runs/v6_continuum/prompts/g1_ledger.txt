LESSONS:
- Capacity versus bandwidth is a trade-off no one has beaten. SRAM chips give high bandwidth but only MB to tens of GB (230 MB, 47.5 MB, 44 GB). HBM chips give hundreds of GB at about 22 TB/s, and that supply is sold out.
- The binding limits are HBM supply, CoWoS packaging, and power per package (about 2.3 kW). These are not removed by a better core design.
- Software has killed otherwise sound hardware (Gaudi missed its $500M target). A new chip must run existing PyTorch/XLA models at no extra porting cost.
- Niche approaches (analog, PIM) fail on precision or programmability. They only help the kernels they were built for.
- Startups ran out of runway (Graphcore, Mythic) or were absorbed (Groq). A credible design must be buildable on existing foundry, HBM and CoWoS capacity.
