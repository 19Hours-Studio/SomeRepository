This works from step 0 instead of relying on a fuzzy match on a learned hidden state.

Code reuses identifiers, keywords and whole phrases over and over, and this head gives exact long-range copying from step one. Indentation, dedents and aligned repeated statements are mostly decided by the character directly above, one line up, so one learned bias entry lets a head look straight up. Plain 1D positions cannot express this, because line lengths vary. It sits on the parent's shifted-key induction heads, with half the heads keyed on h[s-1].
- r0_blindA_2: 1.8694 bits/char (at 2x context 2.5408), 146592 params, 154.9s training, 1 seed(s).
